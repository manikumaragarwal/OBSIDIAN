# Instagram Reel Script — _Mitigating Malicious Envy_ (≈60 seconds)

**🎵 Background:** _I Took a Pill in Ibiza_ (instrumental)

---

### [0:00–0:05]

**(Montage of old photos/videos: failed exams, unfinished projects, rejection emails, awkward clips.)**

> **"What if hiding your failures is actually making people like you less?"**

---

### [0:05–0:13]

> "Have you noticed something lately?"

> "Everyone's suddenly posting their failures."

> "At first, I thought it was just another trend..."

> "...but there's actually psychology behind it."

---

### [0:13–0:24]

> "Researchers at Harvard Business School found something fascinating."

> "When people only see your success, they assume it came effortlessly."

> "They don't see the rejection."

> "Or the failures."

> "The sleepless nights."

> mai ye khud raat ke 2 baje record kar rha hu... but not because i'm hardworking.. i'm just a Nocturnal animal.

---

### [0:24–0:38]

> "That assumption can trigger something called **malicious envy**."

> "Instead of thinking, _'I want what they have,'_"

> "people are more likely to think, _'I hope they lose it.'_"

---

### [0:38–0:50]

> "But here's the twist."

> "When you share the failed attempts..."

> "The awkward first drafts..."

> "The roadblocks..."

> "...people stop seeing arrogance."

> "They start seeing effort."

---

### [0:50–1:00]

**(Return to montage of your failures, then look into the camera.)**

> "Your achievements don't become less impressive."

> "They become more human."

> "So maybe we should stop treating our lives like LinkedIn profiles..."

> "...and start showing the mountain we had to climb to get there."
