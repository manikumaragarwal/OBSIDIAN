Why is everyone suddenly interested in showing theirs failures... on an platform which thrives by the **creators** showing off their PIE, while the **consumers** are yearning to get the piece of that pie.

A team of researchers from Harvard Business school wanted to know what this instinct actually costs us socially, and wheter opening up abou thte messy road to success changes how people feel about us?

Researchers of harvard Business school wanted to know exactly this, does sharing you pile of losses before winning, with other changes how other people feel about us?

they found out, that if you only share your success, people infer hubiristic pride from you. their brain naturally assumes that the achievements came effortlessly.

this triggers a destructive human emotion - _malicious envy_. the subconcious desire to see a succesfull person fall back down to our own level.

But... the researchers discovered a counterintuitive hack, Sharing the brutal roadblocks, the rejections.. changes the interpreteation from arrogance to authentic pride.

The most interesting thing is that... in people's eye, they still admire you... it doesn't lower the perceived value of your wins...
