# Stylistic TRAP of  the Book
 Gulliver's Travel Book -> "*A trap for the Readers*"  in the following questions : **How Swift uses travel genere to secure "==Verisimilitude==(quality of appearing real or plausible)" ?** 
- During this time "Travel Vlog" kind of texts were pretty popular. (e.g. William Dampier used to write dry record of his overseas journey's) and people devoured these texts, because it was the only source for people to know about this expanding world, and Swift uses this method to lure people into reading his fictional story which on a deeper level is just pure criticism towards his government and also other ideas.
- Swift intentionally bury the reader into a mundane character's past, his wife, his education and all that crap, before telling about the giants, and the liliputians, because he wants the reader to read this text like any other "travel blog" they were reading at that time. 
- **The Roayal Society of London** popularized the idea of "***Plain STYLE***"  which says that true objective writing must have no metaphor, but raw data,   so Johnathan swift uses Dead Pan vocabulary to manipulate his audience into really trusting Gulliver's as a reliable narrator, and make them think that they're reading a real Travel Log.  the book has published with "Samuel Gullivers" as the name of author instead of GUlliver's travel to really manipulate the audience further... 
- he uses these rigid form of facts, to share these devastating fictional truth about the society.  the satire works because it doesn't break at any point... (for eg: a mordern mocumentary... the jokes, the satire, the critique only works because everything is played out with complete seriousness, if the characters laughed or looked at the camera, it won't work)
