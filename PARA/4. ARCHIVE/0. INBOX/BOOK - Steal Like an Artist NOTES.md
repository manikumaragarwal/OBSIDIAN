# BOOK - Steal Like an Artist NOTES

> It’s one of my theories that when people give you advice, they’re really just talking to themselves in the past. 
- think about this yourself... whenever we give advice to other people... who is the frame of reference for which we are giving the advice...  
- You don't know what the person actually feels or go through in real life, because you're not inside his brain.... so you just think about yourself.. what you would have felt in that situation... and then you give advice to yourself, or the imaginary version of you going through the same problem he's going through.

### How to see the world Like an artist ? 

> How does an artist look at the world? 
> First, you figure out what’s worth stealing, then you move on to the next thing. 
> That’s about all there is to it. 


> “*The only art I’ll ever study is stuff that I can steal from.*” —  David Bowie 
- The only reason behind my doomscrolling is that I want to steal stuff from other people and package it in a different way, and serve it to other people with my own name.
- *To be an artist, you have to be pretentious*... because you know the art you're proudly calling yours.. came from someone else... who himself stole it from someone else... so how can you not feel pretentious when you're just tweaking an idea preserved throughout generations, and give it a new wrapper saying, it's yours.
