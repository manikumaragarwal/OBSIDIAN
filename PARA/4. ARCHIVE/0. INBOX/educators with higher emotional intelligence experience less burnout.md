# educators with higher emotional intelligence experience less burnout

## Core Finding
Teachers and educational staff with higher emotional intelligence have significantly better professional outcomes.

## The Research Data

### Professional Satisfaction
- More positive about teaching
- Greater job satisfaction
- Less likely to leave the profession

### Administrative Support
- Receive more support from their principals
- Better relationships with leadership

### Well-being
- Experience less burnout
- More sustainable careers
- Better able to handle the emotional demands of teaching

## The Classroom Impact
When teachers have higher EI:
- Students in their classrooms are more engaged
- Better quality relationships between teacher and students
- Students become more pro-social
- Students perform better academically

## The Climate Study
Video analysis of classroom interactions showed:
- Teachers with more positive non-verbal behavior
- Teachers who regard students' perspectives
- Teachers who bring students into the learning environment
- Teachers who use less cynicism and sarcasm
- **Result**: Better student outcomes across the board
