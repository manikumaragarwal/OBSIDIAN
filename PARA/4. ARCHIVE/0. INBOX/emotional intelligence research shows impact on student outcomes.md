# emotional intelligence research shows impact on student outcomes

## Core Finding
Students with higher emotional intelligence consistently demonstrate better outcomes across multiple domains.

## The Research Data

### Mental Health
- Less anxiety
- Less depression

### Risk Behaviors
- Less likely to abuse alcohol in middle/high school
- Less likely to abuse drugs
- Less likely to smoke cigarettes

### Social Behavior
- Less aggressive
- Less likely to engage in bullying behavior
- Perceived as better leaders by teachers and peers
- More attentive in class
- Less hyperactive

### Academic Performance
- Higher grades overall
- Better able to manage challenging academic moments
- Can demonstrate cognitive ability when not emotionally oppressed

## The Critical Insight
You cannot show your academic or cognitive ability when:
- Feeling oppressed
- Feeling unvalued
- Experiencing strong unpleasant emotions

Emotional intelligence provides the foundation for academic success.

## The Gareth Example
A boy who was failing 5th and 6th grade due to horrific bullying:
- Entered an EI-focused middle school
- Developed emotional skills
- Wrote a powerful poem about bullying
- Received standing ovation from peers
- Now in high school, wants to be a poet
- **Demonstrates**: Emotional safety enables academic achievement
