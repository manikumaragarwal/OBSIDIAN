# The Law of Religion - Sapiens BOOK NOTES

- Three great unifiers of Humankind : 
	1. Religion
	2. Money
	3. Empires

- Religion must have these 4 things:
	1. superhuman order (someone greater than human made the rules, human can't change the rules.)
	2. The orders must be so that it unifies people,  Binds people together (no division)
	3. universal superhuman orders - true always and everywhere  
	4. Missionary - everyone should be expanding this order, it shouldn't be kept in a single community or group.
	- *all relegions follow the first two rules, but only few follow the 3rd and the 4th rule (universal and missionary).  e.g - islam and buddhism*
	- *at first when religions started forming, most of them were local and had no intention of expanding to other groups..*

### Silencing of Lambs
Types of belief systems : 
1. Animism : everything have soul, and everything have equal importance, nothing is more important than other. 
		*Humans aren't more important htan plants, just because we eat them...   A tiger isn't more important than human just because it eats us... we all have our own role, and have equal importance in this world.*
2. Polytheism : Many gods for different things,  
		*god of fertility, god of sky, god of war.
3. Monotheism : One single omnipotent god. 


- Animism was seen in local religions, this supernatural order was very important for a particular place, so that people can follow rules for their place, what plants to eat, what animals to hunt or not hunt, etc.  since people would not travel for more than 1000km in their entire life, these orders were not needed to be explain to people of other distant tribe.   

> *The Agricultural Revolution was also accompanied by a Religious Revolution*
- Animism turned out to be a pain in the ass of farmers,  because acc. to Animism, everything had equal rights,  but farmers didnt' give a rat's ass about plants or animals.   
- so, a new belief system emerged,  ***Polytheism***.
		when farmers started seeing animals and plants as their property, they found out soon, that they didn't really have any control over their possesion, so they started believing that there are more powerful beings controlling these forces. 
> **Polytheism arose because farmers needed powerful gods to control different parts of nature that humans depended on but couldn't control themselves.**

Harari argues that **the Agricultural Revolution changed religion because humans stopped seeing themselves as partners with nature and started seeing themselves as masters of nature, relying on powerful gods to control the parts of nature they still couldn't control**. 

> **polytheism** exalted the status of god, as well as of Humans. 
	because, it formed a connection between god and humans, the nature became a side character,  the relation between god and human determines the fate of the entire Nature ecosystem. (god got angry on human, excessive rain, all plants die)
