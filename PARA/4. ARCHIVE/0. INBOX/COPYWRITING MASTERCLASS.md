# Full Note

copywriting - persuades a person to take actions

direct response COPY - immediate action
delayed response (brand) COPY - build brand, or action over time!

normal writing - doesn't have to help them take immediate actions.

---

## How people make actions?

give a solution to the problem, not what you're going to do (no fancy terms!)

hit them in the emotional core of their brain!

### entry ways to get inside reader's mind:

1. Desires
2. Notions
3. Identifications
4. Characteristics

#### DESIRES

- **desires are important for writing content, or selling something... it's what drives a person.. it's what's people want... its very important to know your target audience's desires... what do they need! then give them what they need.**

desired ROD:

- fancy job
- lot of respect!
- outer factors!

> _Unlock the secrets to landing your dream position at a top tier company, earning the respect and recognition you deserve, without sacrificing who you are_

desired SHAWN:

- build something of own
- self fulfilliment while building
- not outer factors!

> _design your career on your terms, break free from the conventional, and build something that reflects your unique version -- No permission required._

#### NOTIONS

- **beliefs your audience have about the world, this is why some copies feel more relatable... also you can use their notions to make yourself feel more trustworthy.. since you TRUELY UNDERSTAND them.**

#### IDENTIFICATIONS

- ** labels and values, people have given to themselves over their life...and peopel have very strong affiliations with these values and identities... leading to a strong emotional connection..**

- we can weave these connections (identities) in our copies, to make the reader feel SEEN, more UNDERSTOOD...

### 1. DESIRES

- SUPERFICIAL : surface level desires
  - looking better, earning more money, better sleep, , more time, etc.
  - delicate : backpain, encourage a picky child to eat more.
- DEEP desires :

DEEP desires are the subconscious desires underlying superficial desires that people have,

superficial desire : "_I want to be rich_"
deep desire:
I want my parents to respect me.
I want to proof myself that I'm not a looser.
I want to have freedom to choose things.

> [!NOTE] same superficial desire might be caused by different deep desires for different people!

### PROMISES

> _"simple statements, that SAY or IMPLY, that you can do the DESIRE that your prospect have."_

What makes a **GOOD PROMISE** ?

1. Benefit-Driven
2. Clear and Easy to Understand
3. Believable and Provable
4. Promise aligns with ultimate Call to Action

> _A copywriters job is not to sell attention! it's to grab the attention and use that attention into Action or Engagement._

MISTAKES to avoid in your copy:

1. appeal the prospect straight away
2. don't clutter your message with jargons.
