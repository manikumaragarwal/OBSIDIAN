## Would this Idea be Possible from an Economic and Caloric perspective ? 

#### 1. [[Johnathan Swift]]'s Key Assumptions (from text)
- Child at 1 year old → **28 pounds** ≈ **12.7 kg** (well-nursed, plump)
- Nursing cost → **≈ 2 shillings** per annum (mainly extra food for mother)
- Sale price → **10 shillings** for carcass (profit 8 shillings)
- Meat → "delicious", 4 dishes per child (quarters), luxury for rich

#### 2. Mother's Extra Calorie Input (Pregnancy + 1 Year Breastfeeding)
**Pregnancy (≈ 9 months / 270–280 days)**

- Extra kcal/day: ≈ **300** average (≈ 0 first trimester, 340 second, 450 third)
- Total extra → 300 × 280 ≈ **84,000 kcal** (rough) Or more accurately: **80,000 – 100,000 kcal**

**Breastfeeding (1 year / 365 days, exclusive/near-exclusive)**
- Extra kcal/day needed: **330–500 kcal** (most sources 400–500) → Conservative: 400 kcal/day → Total → 400 × 365 = **146,000 kcal** → Range: **120,000 – 182,500 kcal**

**Grand Total extra calories mother consumes** = Pregnancy + Lactation ≈ **200,000 – 280,000 kcal** (typical estimate 220–250k)

#### Edible Calories in 1-Year-Old Child (≈12.7 kg)
**Human tissue caloric density** (edible parts: muscle + fat + organs)
- Pure fat → **9 kcal/g**
- But real body: muscle ≈ 4–5 kcal/g (protein), mix with water/bone
- Realistic edible human density → **≈ 1,400 – 2,200 kcal/kg** (from adult scaling studies) (Average adult male ≈ 1,800–2,000 kcal/kg edible after waste)

**For plump infant** (higher fat %, but more water, smaller muscles → slightly lower efficiency)
- Optimistic: 2,000 kcal/kg × 12.7 kg = **25,400 kcal**
- Realistic range: **1,500 – 2,000 kcal/kg** → **19,000 – 25,400 kcal**
- Very generous (treat like fatty pork): **≈ 2,500 kcal/kg** → **≈ 32,000 kcal** max

**Best-case edible yield** → **≈ 25,000 – 40,000 kcal** (most estimates closer to 30k)

#### 4. Net Caloric Balance (Thermodynamic Feasibility)

Input (mother extra) → **≈ 220,000 – 250,000 kcal** Output (child edible) → **≈ 25,000 – 35,000 kcal**

**Net loss** → **≈ 85–90% energy wasted** → **Child provides only 10–15% of calories mother invested** → Massive caloric **deficit** for society/family → Better to just eat extra food directly or feed to pigs (pigs convert feed → meat much more efficiently)

**Efficiency comparison**
- Human child → very poor "crop"
- Livestock (pig/cow) → 20–40% feed-to-edible conversion
- Conclusion: Even calorie-wise → **complete failure** (not sustainable food source)


### 5. Economic Quick Math (Swift's Logic vs Reality)

**Cost side**
- Nursing → 2 shillings ≈ very cheap (extra maternal food + rags)

**Revenue side**
- Sale → 10 shillings
- Profit → 8 shillings per child (≈ 400% markup)

**But hidden costs (not in essay)**
- Lost future worker (child would grow → labor/tax value)
- No population growth → long-term economic collapse
- Market? → Very niche (rich only), limited demand
- Calories → Not worth it (see above)

**Verdict in numbers** 
Profit per child → Yes on paper (8 shillings) But net societal energy/food → Huge **loss** → Economic "win" is illusion – satire shows how absurd cold calculations are

**Final notebook takeaway** 
Even setting aside morals, the proposal is **thermodynamically stupid** (big energy loss) and **economically shortsighted** (short-term cash, long-term ruin).
