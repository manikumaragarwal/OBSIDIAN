1. Discuss the Sonnet : 
	**Sonnet** : 
	- Octet : 8 lines.[ABBA ABBA]  (poet express his doubts and suffering)
	- Sestet : 6 lines.[CDE CDE]  (poet accepts his suffering & gives conclusion to his suffering)

2. Write a short note on Milton's **Personification**.
	**Personification** : Giving Human Characteristics to a non-living entity or thing.
	Poet's murmur (**patience**) is personified as a character who interrupts him, when his brain is swirling with questions and Doubts.

3. What is Central Theme of The Poem ?
	1. **Patience** v/s **Action** : learning that waiting and accepting can be as valuable as doing.
	2. **Service to God** : What does it really mean to serve? is it about works or faith?
	3. **Disability and Worth** : can someone still have value/purpose when they lose an ability? 
	4. **Divine Justice** : Questioning wheter God's expectation are fair. 
	5. **Acceptance** : Moving from complaint to peaceful accpetance of one's circumstances.
