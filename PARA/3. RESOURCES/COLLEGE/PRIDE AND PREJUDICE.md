> [!NOTE] Aristotelian Plot Structure in Pride & Prejudice
> Austen follows [[Aristotle's Poetics|Aristotelian plot principles]] — causality, unity of action, beginning/middle/end:
> - **Complication** (Rising Action): Darcy's pride + Elizabeth's prejudice → misunderstandings multiply
> - **Peripeteia** ([[3 Tragic Beats]]): Darcy's first rejected proposal — his fortune and status fail to win Elizabeth
> - **Anagnorisis** ([[3 Tragic Beats]]): "Till this moment I never knew myself" — Elizabeth realises her own prejudice
> - Pride & Prejudice as comedy follows the same structural logic as tragedy — the beats are simply inverted toward resolution


# CRITICAL COMMENTARIES
##### IRONY throughout the novel - 
1. "Everyone knows that a single rich man is looking for a wife"  is an irony.  
	- In reality, "Every single rich man knows, he is to be persuaded by women, who want to be his wife".
2. In a novel, about couple overcoming misunderstandings to achieve a marital status, the first view of marriage is an mismatched couple, who can't communicate.
	- Mr. and Mrs. Bennet are polar opposites to each other:  Mr. Bennet is a dry witted man, who uses sarcasms throughout the novel, and Mrs. Bennet misses all those mocking tones.
3. Elizabeth criticises Jane for being blind, that she see's good in other people as a reflection her own heart. That she should be more careful.
	- It's ironic how Elizabeth is herself blinded by appearances, and prejudice.

### Elizabeth is an exception from the women in her times
1. Elizabeth is quick witted, confident, and intelligent.
	- during her times, girl were a little introverted, less spoken. but Elizabeth is sharp with her words, she is quick witted, and she knows how to defend herself in a conversation.
2. Elizabeth enjoys physical activity, especially walking.
	- when Elizabeth easily walks 3 miles to visit her sick sister, caroline bingley's snide response shows that this behaviour was not common among gentlewomen of her time.

> [!TIP] Elizabeth Bennet as a Parallel Character Across Texts
> Elizabeth shares qualities with other rational women who challenge patriarchal norms:
> - **[[Lysistrata - Aristophanes|Lysistrata]]** — both are witty, rational women who expose masculine failures
> - **[[Antigone - Sophocles|Antigone]]** — both defy social expectations out of principle, but Elizabeth operates within the system strategically while Antigone confronts it head-on
> - **[[TARA play|Tara's Bharati]]** — contrast: Bharati is silenced by patriarchy; Elizabeth finds a voice within it

## MOST LIKELY ASKED QUESTIONS : 
1. What are the themes of marriage? And which marriage is considered best by austen. 
	- Philosophy of Austen's on marriage :  **Psychological egalitarian marriage**  (compatibility)
- TYPES OF MARRIAGE :
	1. *Mr. and Mrs. Bennet.* => **Marriage based on crush or infatuation** (mrs bennet is dumb, mr. bennet spends most time in library)
	2. *Charlotte and Collins* => **married because the conventional parameters were met**. => Charlotte says, she's not interested in the romantic idea of marriage.
	3. *Lydia and Wickham* => **she was very haste.** (she elopes)
	4. *Jane and Bingley* => they attracted to each other (both partner's are not compatible because they can't express their emotions properly, Bingley leaves her on someone else's words. )
	5. *Elizabeth and Darcy* => BEST MARRIAGE => 
		- Lizzy not governed by conventional notion. (==subordination of women==)
		- notions => **accomplishments** of women. 

> [!NOTE] Types of Marriage — Compare Across Texts
> Austen's marriage types parallel questions of dharma and duty in other texts:
> - **Charlotte & Collins** (pragmatic marriage) parallels how [[Mahabharata|Draupadi]] is 'married' to five husbands as a political arrangement — convention over ideal
> - **Lydia & Wickham** (rash marriage) parallels how impulsive decisions lead to tragedy in [[Doctor Faustus - Christopher Marlowe|Doctor Faustus]]
> - **Elizabeth & Darcy** (ideal psychological egalitarian marriage) — see also [[Building your Personal Brand - Personal Notes|Personal Brand theory]]: Darcy's transformation is essentially a *rebranding* — he had to prove his "core truths" through action, not just status
