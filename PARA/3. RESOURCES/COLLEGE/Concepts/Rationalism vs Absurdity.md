# Rationalism vs Absurdity

A key dialectic in [[18th Century English Literature]], especially in [[Gulliver's Travels - Jonathan Swift]]: the [[Enlightenment Thought|Enlightenment's]] confidence in reason is tested against the absurdity of how reason is actually practised.

## The Tension

- Reason as ideal vs reason misapplied
- Rational projects that produce irrational outcomes
- The absurdity of taking reason to its logical extreme (Houyhnhnms)
- Swift's argument that institutional reason can become tyranny

## In Gulliver's Travels

- **Laputa** — mathematicians so abstract they need "flappers" to remind them to speak
- **Balnibarbi** — a land ruined by rational "improvement" schemes
- **Houyhnhnms** — perfect reason without emotion or compassion
- The Academy of Lagado — extracting sunbeams from cucumbers
