# Cosmopolitanism

Cosmopolitanism is the ethical and political idea that all human beings belong to a single community, transcending national, ethnic, or cultural boundaries.



## Key Ideas

- Universal moral obligations beyond one's own nation
- Rational sympathy for distant peoples
- Critique of narrow patriotism and parochialism
- Tension between cosmopolitan ideals and imperial practice

## In 18th Century Literature

- [[Jonathan Swift]] exposes European arrogance through alien perspectives in [[Gulliver's Travels - Jonathan Swift]]
- The King of Brobdingnag's revulsion at European civilisation is a cosmopolitan critique
- [[Oliver Goldsmith]] explores cosmopolitan identity in *The Citizen of the World*
- [[Alexander Pope]] — "An Essay on Man" gestures toward universal human nature
