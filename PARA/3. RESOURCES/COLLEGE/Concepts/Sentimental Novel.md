# Sentimental Novel

The sentimental novel is a prose fiction form that foregrounds emotional response, sympathy, and moral feeling as central to its narrative purpose. It flourished in the mid-to-late eighteenth century.

## Key Features

- Idealised characters who display benevolence and sensibility
- Plots driven by misfortune, suffering, and eventual moral vindication
- Appeal to the reader's sympathetic emotions (tears, pity)
- Moral lesson embedded in emotional engagement

## Appears In

- [[The Vicar of Wakefield]] — paradigmatic example of the sentimental novel
- Samuel Richardson's *Pamela* and *Clarissa*
- Laurence Sterne's *A Sentimental Journey*
