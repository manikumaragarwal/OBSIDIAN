# Zeugma

Zeugma is a figure of speech in which a single word (usually a verb or adjective) governs two or more words in different senses, creating witty juxtaposition.

## Example in Pope

> "Or stain her honour, or her new brocade" — [[The Rape of the Lock]], II.107

Here "stain" applies to both *honour* (abstract) and *brocade* (concrete), equating moral ruin with fabric damage — a brilliantly compact [[Satire|satirical]] move.

## Effect

- Compresses wit into minimal space
- Exposes conflation of values (honour = dress)
- Central to [[Alexander Pope|Pope's]] style in the [[Heroic Couplet]]

## More Examples

> "Or lose her heart, or necklace, at a ball" (II.109)
> "Dost sometimes counsel take — and sometimes tea" (III.8)
