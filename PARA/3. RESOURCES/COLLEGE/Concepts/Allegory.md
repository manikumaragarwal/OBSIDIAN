# Allegory

Allegory is an extended metaphor in which characters, events, and settings represent abstract ideas or moral/political meanings beneath the surface narrative.

## Types

- **Political allegory** — fictional events map to real political situations
- **Moral allegory** — characters embody virtues and vices
- **Religious allegory** — narrative parallels spiritual truths

## Appears In

- [[Gulliver's Travels - Jonathan Swift]] — each voyage allegorises aspects of English politics and [[Human Nature in Swift|human nature]]
- [[The Rape of the Lock]] — sylphs allegorise female social reputation
- [[The Vicar of Wakefield]] — the Primrose family's trials as a moral/Christian allegory
