# Public Sphere

The public sphere is a conceptual space between the private household and the state where citizens engage in rational-critical debate about public matters through print, speech, and association.

## Key Ideas

- Emerged in 18th-century coffeehouses, salons, and print culture
- Based on principles of open access and rational argument
- Distinct from both court culture and popular culture
- Foundation of modern democratic discourse

## In Literature

- Writers like [[Alexander Pope]], [[Jonathan Swift]], and [[Oliver Goldsmith]] shaped and were shaped by the public sphere
- Periodicals (*The Tatler*, *The Spectator*) formed its literary infrastructure
- The novel rose as the literary genre of the [[Bourgeois Society|bourgeois]] public
