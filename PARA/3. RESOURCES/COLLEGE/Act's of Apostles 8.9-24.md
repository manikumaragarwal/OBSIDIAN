In the City of Samaria, a man names **Simon**, had the ability to perform Magic, and people were so amazed by him that they called him "*The Great Power of God*". 

He had Status, Followers and Brand.

**Philip the Evangelist**(one of the seven men appointed by apostles in Jerusalem to Spread Christianity beyond Jewish Circle) arrives in the City of Samaria, and start preaching the knowledge of Christianity, and people start believing and started getting baptized. 

Simon himself gets baptised, he was impressed by the power of the Apostles after he saw that  *When Apostles lay their hand of believers, they get Holy Spirit.*

Simon saw this power, and approached **Peter the Apostle** with his offer : "In exchange of money, Give me this ability, so that whoever I lay my hand on receives Holy spirit."

Apostle reponds sharply, that "Your heart is not right before God, Your silvers will perish with you, because you thought You could buy god's fit with your Money."

Simon, shaken, asks Peter to pray for him so that none of the judgment Peter spoke of will happen to him.



# Themes
Simon’s “magic” amazed people. The apostles’ work pointed away from themselves toward God. One draws attention to the performer; the other to the source.
