Aristotle sees the Poetical process as an 'Imitation'([[Mimesis]]) of real life situations, rather than inventions.

Since, Poetical process is a metal [[abstraction]] of many individual situations, it is 'truer' than any single situation, more 'universal', more 'general', or (as Plato might have said)[[Theory of Forms - Plato| it participates in the Ideal to a greater degree]]
