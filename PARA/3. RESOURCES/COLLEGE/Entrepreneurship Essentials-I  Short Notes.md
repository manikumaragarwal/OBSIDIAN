# UNIT 1: INTRODUCTION TO ENTREPRENEURSHIP & OPPORTUNITY RECOGNITION

_(16 hours – VERY IMPORTANT UNIT)_

---

## 1. What is Entrepreneurship?

### Definition (Exam-ready)

Entrepreneurship is the **process of identifying opportunities**, mobilising resources, **creating value through innovation**, and **bearing risk** to start and grow a venture.

📌 Quote-friendly idea (not compulsory):

> Entrepreneurship is not just about starting businesses, but about creating value.

---

## 2. Importance of Entrepreneurship

Write in **points**, not paragraphs:

- Economic growth
    
- Employment generation
    
- Innovation & technological progress
    
- Regional development
    
- Wealth creation
    
- Social change (social entrepreneurship)
    

---

## 3. Why Be an Entrepreneur?

Examiner expects **motivation + outcomes**:

- Independence & autonomy
    
- Financial rewards
    
- Innovation & creativity
    
- Social impact
    
- Self-actualisation
    

💡 Tip: End with

> “Thus, entrepreneurship is both an economic and psychological pursuit.”

---

### Entrepreneurs as Leaders

# UNIT 2: ENTREPRENEURIAL MINDSET

_(Conceptual + Applied = High scoring)_

---

## 1. What is Entrepreneurial Mindset?

An entrepreneurial mindset refers to a **way of thinking that enables individuals to identify opportunities, take initiative, and persist despite challenges**.

---

## 2. Key Dimensions of Entrepreneurial Mindset

- Opportunity orientation
    
- Risk tolerance
    
- Innovation
    
- Learning from failure
    
- Resilience
    
- Ethical judgement
    

📌 Always connect mindset → success.

---

## 3. Growth Mindset vs Fixed Mindset (Dweck)

**Guaranteed short question**

|Fixed Mindset|Growth Mindset|
|---|---|
|Avoids challenges|Embraces challenges|
|Fear of failure|Learns from failure|
|Static abilities|Skills can be developed|

---

## 4. Developing an Entrepreneurial Mindset

Mention practices:

- Reflection
    
- SWOT analysis
    
- Learning from failure
    
- Networking
    
- Continuous learning

## 5. Ethical Considerations in Entrepreneurship

# UNIT 3: BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY IDENTIFICATION

_(Very analytical unit)_

---

## 1. What is a Business Opportunity?

A situation where **customer needs, market demand, and feasibility intersect**.

---

## 2. Idea Generation vs Opportunity

Exam trick question:

- **Idea** = raw thought
    
- **Opportunity** = viable, feasible, profitable idea
    

---

## 3. Opportunity Identification Methods

Write 4–5 points:

- Market gaps
    
- Technological change
    
- Customer pain points
    
- Regulatory changes
    
- Trend analysis
    

---

## 4. Empathy & Design Thinking

🔥 HOT TOPIC

### Design Thinking Stages:

1. Empathise
    
2. Define
    
3. Ideate
    
4. Prototype
    
5. Test
    

Purpose:

- Understand users deeply
    
- Build user-centric solutions

## 5. Social Entrepreneurship
