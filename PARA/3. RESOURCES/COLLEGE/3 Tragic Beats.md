Defined in [[Aristotle's Poetics]] as the three key moments of a tragedy:

1. **Peripeteia** : a sudden reversal of fortune.
2. **Anagnorisis** : When a character makes a critical discovery
3. **Catharsis** : The emotional relief in an audience.

> [!NOTE] Cross-Text Examples
> These three beats appear in almost every drama studied in class. See [[Aristotle's Poetics]] for the theoretical foundation.

### Examples of the three tragic beats.

##### 1. Midsommar : 
1. Peripeteia : when Dany was crowned as May Queen. 
	- the reversal of fortune,  as Dany was just a tourist, but suddently get's the highest place at the table because she won the dance competition.
2. Anagnorisis : When Dany discovers Chris's infidelity.
	- Dany makes a discovery... that her boyfriend is cheating on her...  
	- [[Altough, there are many discoveries throughout the movies, would all be considered Anagnorisis?]]
3. Catharsis : When Dany grieves with other girls.
	- It's an emotional release after this dark film.

##### 2. [[Mahabharata]] :
1. **Peripeteia** : Yudhishthira loses everything in the dice game — a catastrophic reversal.
2. **Anagnorisis** : Draupadi's devastating question — did Yudhishthira lose himself first or her? See [[Draupadi is often seen as 'Nathabati Anathabath' or 'Five lords', Yet none a 'Protector'. Discuss]]
3. **Catharsis** : Krishna's peace mission — the audience feels the full weight of the war's inevitability.

##### 3. [[Doctor Faustus - Christopher Marlowe|Doctor Faustus]] :
1. **Peripeteia** : Faustus's damnation despite last-minute pleas — the pact sealed his fate.
2. **Anagnorisis** : Faustus realises in the final hour that he wasted 24 years of power on trivial pranks.
3. **Catharsis** : The audience feels the tragic waste of great potential.

##### 4. [[TARA play|Tara]] (Mahesh Dattani):
1. **Peripeteia** : Dan discovers the truth about his sister's leg — his entire identity collapses.
2. **Anagnorisis** : The audience discovers along with Dan what really happened in the hospital.
3. **Catharsis** : Dan writing the play is his attempt to process grief and guilt.

##### 5. [[Antigone - Sophocles|Antigone]]:
1. **Peripeteia** : Creon's rigid authority leads to everyone he loves dying.
2. **Anagnorisis** : Creon realises his hubris destroyed his family — too late.
3. **Catharsis** : The audience experiences the pity and terror of divine justice.

##### 6. [[Macbeth - William Shakespeare|Macbeth]]:
1. **Peripeteia** : Macbeth's decision to murder Duncan — reversal from noble warrior to murderous tyrant
2. **Anagnorisis** : The "Tomorrow and tomorrow" soliloquy — Macbeth realizes ambition was meaningless
3. **Catharsis** : The audience feels both pity (for a noble man destroyed by weakness) and terror (of the violence within us all)
