> [!NOTE] Aristotelian Framework for the Mahabharata
> The Mahabharata is a perfect case study for [[Aristotle's Poetics|Aristotelian tragedy]]:
> - **Peripeteia**: Yudhishthira's dice loss — sudden, catastrophic reversal of fortune
> - **Anagnorisis**: Draupadi's question piercing through the illusion of dharmic authority
> - **Catharsis**: The inevitability of the Kurukshetra war evokes pity and terror
> See [[3 Tragic Beats]] for this framework applied across texts.

> [!TIP] Dharma vs. Heroic Ideal
> The Mahabharata critiques the heroic ideal much like [[Lysistrata - Aristophanes|Lysistrata]] critiques the glorification of war — both show how masculine pride leads to collective destruction. Compare also with [[Antigone - Sophocles|Antigone]]'s examination of duty vs. authority.
