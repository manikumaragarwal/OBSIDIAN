### **1. DSC-4: 14th–17th Century English Poetry**

**Focus:** How English poetry evolves from medieval to early modern times.

- **Chaucer** – _General Prologue_, _Pardoner’s Tale_  
    → Society, class, hypocrisy, medieval worldview
    
- **Philip Sidney** – _Sonnet I_  
    → Sonnet tradition, love, poetic self-awareness
    
- **Walter Raleigh** – _The Passionate Man’s Pilgrimage_  
    → Death, faith, morality
    
- **John Donne** – _The Sun Rising_, _The Canonization_, _The Good Morrow_  
    → Metaphysical poetry: love, intellect, paradox
    
- **John Milton** – _Paradise Lost_, Book I  
    → Epic poetry, rebellion, free will, religion

### **2. DSC-5: 16th–17th Century English Drama**

**Focus:** Theatre as a space of power, conflict, and experimentation.

**You study:**

- **Christopher Marlowe** – _Doctor Faustus_  
    → Ambition, knowledge, sin
    
- **William Shakespeare** – _Macbeth_  
    → Power, guilt, fate, ambition
    
- **Aphra Behn** – _The Rover_  
    → Gender, desire, Restoration drama
    

**What examiners want:**  
Character analysis, dramatic structure, social criticism.

---

### **3. DSC-6: 18th Century Literature**

**Focus:** The “Age of Reason” and the rise of satire, realism, and the novel.

**You study:**

- **Alexander Pope** – _The Rape of the Lock_  
    → Satire, mock-epic, society & manners
    
- **Jonathan Swift** – _Gulliver’s Travels_  
    → Political satire, human folly
    
- **Oliver Goldsmith** – _The Vicar of Wakefield_  
    → Early novel, morality, family
    

**What examiners want:**  
Satire, enlightenment ideas, social change.
