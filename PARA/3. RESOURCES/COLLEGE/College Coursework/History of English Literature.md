## 📜 History of English Literature 

| **Age / Period**                             | **Years**    | **Reason / Background**              | **Main Features**                                     | **Major Writers**                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[[Anglo-Saxon (Old English) Period]]**     | 450–1066     | Germanic tribes settled England      | Heroic poetry, alliteration, pagan + Christian themes | Beowulf (anonymous)                                                                       |
| **[[Middle English Period]]**                | 1066–1500    | Norman Conquest                      | Religious & secular writing, allegory                 | Geoffrey Chaucer, William Langland                                                        |
| **[[Renaissance / Early Modern Age]]**       | 1500–1660    | Rebirth of classical learning        | Humanism, revival of drama & poetry                   | Sir Philip Sidney, Edmund Spenser, Thomas More                                            |
| **[[Elizabethan Age (Age of Shakespeare)]]** | 1558–1603    | Reign of Queen Elizabeth I           | Golden age of drama, blank verse                      | William Shakespeare, Christopher Marlowe, Ben Jonson                                      |
| **[[Jacobean Age]]**                         | 1603–1625    | Reign of James I                     | Dark tragedies, pessimism                             | Shakespeare (later plays), John Webster                                                   |
| **[[Caroline Age]]**                         | 1625–1649    | Reign of Charles I                   | Refined poetry, Metaphysical poetry                   | John Donne, George Herbert, Robert Herrick                                                |
| **[[Puritan / Commonwealth Age]]**           | 1649–1660    | Rule of Oliver Cromwell              | Religious & moral literature, decline of drama        | John Milton, Andrew Marvell                                                               |
| **[[Restoration Age]]**                      | 1660–1700    | Restoration of monarchy (Charles II) | Comedy of manners, satire, realism                    | John Dryden, William Wycherley                                                            |
| **[Augustan / Neo-Classical Age]]**          | 1700–1745    | Inspired by Roman writers            | Reason, order, satire, heroic couplet                 | Alexander Pope, [[Johnathan Swift]]                                                       |
| **[[Age of Sensibility]]**                   | 1745–1798    | Emotional transition period          | Sentimentality, early nature poetry                   | Thomas Gray, Oliver Goldsmith                                                             |
| **[[Romantic Age]]**                         | 1798–1837    | Reaction against reason              | Imagination, nature, individualism                    | William Wordsworth, Samuel Taylor Coleridge, Lord Byron, Percy Bysshe Shelley, John Keats |
| **[[Victorian Age]]**                        | 1837–1901    | Reign of Queen Victoria              | Morality, realism, social issues                      | Charles Dickens, Alfred Tennyson, Robert Browning, George Eliot                           |
| **[[Modern Age]]**                           | 1901–1945    | World Wars, industrial impact        | Psychetological realism, experimentation              | Virginia Woolf, T. S. Eliot, James Joyce, W. B. Yeats                                     |
| **[[Post-Modern Age]]**                      | 1945–present | After World War II                   | Fragmentation, multiple voices                        | Samuel Beckett, Salman Rushdie                                                            |
