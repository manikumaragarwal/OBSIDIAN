# Ariel (Rape of the Lock)

Ariel is the chief sylph in [[The Rape of the Lock]], a supernatural guardian of [[Belinda (Rape of the Lock)|Belinda's]] honour and beauty.

## Character

- Commands a squadron of sylphs tasked with protecting Belinda
- Appears to Belinda in a dream warning of danger
- Powerless to prevent the Baron's act when Belinda harbours "an earthly lover"
- Modelled on epic divine figures (Athena, Juno) but in [[Mock Epic|miniature]]

## Significance

- The sylphs are [[Allegory|allegorical]] — they represent social reputation and the invisible forces of fashion
- Their failure exposes the fragility of female "honour" → [[PARA/3. RESOURCES/COLLEGE/DSC-6/Themes/Gender Politics]]
- Ariel's machinery parodies the divine interventions of classical epic

## Key Quotation

> "Know further yet; whoever fair and chaste / Rejects mankind, is by some sylph embraced" (I.67–68)
