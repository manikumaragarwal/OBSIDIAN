# British Empire in the 18th Century

The eighteenth century marked the rapid expansion of British colonial power across the Atlantic, India, and the Pacific. This imperial context profoundly shaped the literature and politics of the period.

## Key Developments

- Consolidation of colonies in North America and the Caribbean
- East India Company's growing control of Indian subcontinent
- Transatlantic slave trade as an economic engine
- Mercantile capitalism funding imperial expansion
- Seven Years' War (1756–63) establishing British global dominance

## In Literature

- [[Jonathan Swift]] critiques colonialism in [[Gulliver's Travels - Jonathan Swift]], especially the Voyage to the Houyhnhnms
- [[Alexander Pope]] engages with global trade imagery in [[The Rape of the Lock]]
- Goldsmith depicts economic anxieties tied to imperial wealth in [[The Vicar of Wakefield]]
