# Religion in 18th Century England

The religious landscape of eighteenth-century England was shaped by the tension between established Anglicanism, Dissenting traditions, Catholicism, and the rational theology of the [[Enlightenment Thought|Enlightenment]].

## Key Features

- The Church of England as the established church, closely tied to political power
- Penal laws restricting Catholics and Dissenters
- Deist challenges to revealed religion
- Latitudinarianism — a tolerant, rational form of Anglicanism
- Evangelicalism emerging later in the century (Wesley)
