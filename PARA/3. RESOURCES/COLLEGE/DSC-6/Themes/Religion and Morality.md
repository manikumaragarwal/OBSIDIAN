# Religion and Morality

Religion remained a powerful force in eighteenth-century England, but it was increasingly reframed in moral and rational terms by the [[Enlightenment Thought|Enlightenment]].

## Key Tensions

- Deism vs revealed Christianity
- Religion as moral framework vs institutional power
- Toleration debates (Catholic, Dissenter, Anglican)
- Science challenging biblical literalism

## In the Texts

- [[The Rape of the Lock]] — the toilet scene parodies religious ritual; cosmetics become sacred rites
- [[Gulliver's Travels - Jonathan Swift]] — religious wars satirised through Lilliputian Big-Endian/Little-Endian conflict
- [[The Vicar of Wakefield]] — genuine Christian patience as the moral centre of the novel → [[Family Morality]]
