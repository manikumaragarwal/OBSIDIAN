# The Local Arbitrage Play: Using Google Maps And AI Builders To Sell Zero-Friction Web Projects

High-value services can be built on simple data arbitrage. By identifying businesses with high social proof (good reviews) but zero digital presence (no website) on Google Maps, an entrepreneur can use AI builders to create and sell full websites with nearly zero technical overhead.

## Breakdown
1. **Lead Sourcing:** Search Google Maps for local businesses with 4+ star reviews but no website link.
2. **Data Extraction:** Copy their business info, service list, and reviews.
3. **Prompt Generation:** Feed this data into ChatGPT to generate a structured content plan for their site.
4. **AI Assembly:** Use a prompt-to-site AI builder to generate the full website in minutes.
5. **The Sale:** Call the business, show them the already-built site, and sell the solution directly.

## The Why
Local businesses don't buy code; they buy visibility. By building the site *before* the call, you remove the "Risk of Failure" for the business owner. You aren't selling a project; you are selling a finished asset that solves an immediate problem they didn't know how to fix.
