# The 3-Stage Brand Building Model: Moving From Identity To Advocate Loyalty

Branding is the process of moving an audience through four emotional states: Knowing -> Trusting -> Choosing -> Advocating. A brand that wins emotionally will always outperform one that attempts to win purely on logic.

## Breakdown
1. **Brand Identity (Who are you?):** The foundation. Requires clear purpose, values, and personality. If this is fuzzy, all marketing looks random.
2. **Brand Meaning (What do people associate with you?):** Perception based on Functional Benefits (Problem solved) and Emotional Benefits (How they feel).
3. **Brand Response (Judgment stage):** Where consistency matters most. Quality perception, credibility, and trust are built or broken here.

## The Why
Consistency is the engine of trust. One bad experience causes a \"trust dip\" that can reset the entire cycle. Brands are not logos; they are the sum total of emotional associations people have with your identity.
