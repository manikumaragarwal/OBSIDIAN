# The Founder Personal Brand Framework: The 60-30-10 Content Strategy For Building Expert Authority

Building a personal brand as a founder is about solving the \"Trust Gap,\" not getting sponsorships. By following a strict 60/30/10 ratio and focusing on niche perspective, a founder moves from a \"confused face in a pit\" to a ray of hope for their audience.

## Breakdown
1. **The 60/30/10 Ratio:**
   - **60% Value:** Directly solve their problems with actionable advice.
   - **30% Work:** Show you are \"legit\" by sharing your routine, lifestyle, and project progress.
   - **10% Personal:** Share your unique story to build human connection.
2. **Targeting Strategies:**
   - **Option A (Unique Perspective):** Offer non-obvious solutions to common niche topics.
   - **Option B (Underserved Segments):** Take basic advice but talk to a very specific audience (e.g., female marathon runners).
3. **Camera Charisma:** You must mix education with an entertainment side. Education alone is boring; you need character mix to be effective.

## The Why
Confusion is a strategy used by bad actors to sell hope. Founders win by being the \"Ray of Hope\" who simplifies the complexity. Consistency paired with entertainment ensures that your high-value message actually reaches the leads you need.
