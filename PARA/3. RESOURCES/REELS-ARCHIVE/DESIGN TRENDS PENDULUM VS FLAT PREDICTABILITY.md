# DESIGN TRENDS PENDULUM VS FLAT PREDICTABILITY

## The Pendulum Swing of Visual Styles
Design trends don't grow in a straight line; they move like a pendulum. When a visual style becomes ubiquitous, making everything feel too flat, minimal, and predictable, designers and users naturally crave the exact opposite. This reactionary shift is where new trends are born. 

## The Rationale and "Aha!" Moment
The true "Aha!" moment is realizing that copying an emerging trend isn't necessarily a failure of originality—it is an involuntary psychological response to visual fatigue. If you want to create timeless design, you must first understand the systemic pendulum behind these trends. You cannot fight the natural human craving for novelty and the "opposite" of what is currently overused.
