# The Dating Audit: 3 Psychological Questions To Validate Relationship Readiness

Relationship success is a result of internal clarity, not finding the "perfect" partner. Before dating, an individual must answer three critical questions to ensure they are seeking a companion rather than simply a source of validation or attention.

## Breakdown
1. **Attachment Style:** Are you Anxious, Avoidant, Dismissive Avoidant, or Secure? Understanding this reveals your default emotional reaction to intimacy.
2. **Relationship vs. Companion:** Do you want love and validation regardless of the source, or do you want a specific companion to build a meaningful life with?
3. **The Single Contentment Test:** If you were single for the next 3-5 years, would you be happy and content with your life? Contentment is the prerequisite for a healthy partnership.

## The Why
Seeking a relationship from a place of "Lack" leads to addictive attachment patterns. By performing this "Dating Audit," you ensure that your relationship choices are made from a position of "Secure Abundance," where a partner is a multiplier of your existing happiness rather than the sole source of it.
