# The Dalen Playbook: Structured Premium Educational Content

A two-part framework for creating "as good as Daniel Dalen" content by combining a specific three-tier video structure with unique "Points of Interest."

## Breakdown
1. **The Three-Tier Structure**:
    - **Entertainment (The Hook)**: Start with fast-paced jump cuts, match cuts, and hints at authority to stop the scroll.
    - **Documentation (The Middle)**: Keep this section authentic and unscripted. Use very few cuts, but *only* include the most interesting parts of recorded footage.
    - **Education (The End)**: A personal breakdown of recent lessons learned, recorded solo to maintain a personal connection.
2. **Points of Interest (POI)**:
    - Identify unique and memorable traits (e.g., location, languages, business experience).
    - Combine these POIs with the structure to make the content unique and "blow up."

## Tool Stack
- Jump Cuts / Match Cuts (Entertainment)
- Unscripted Footage (Documentation)
- Solo Recording (Education)

## The Why
The framework identifies that "Premium" content is a balance of high-energy attention grabbing and low-energy authentic connection. By segregating these into specific phases, a creator can satisfy the algorithm (Entertainment) while building deep trust (Documentation/Education).

## Script Spark
> "Don't just teach; entertain first to earn the right to document, and document only to earn the right to educate."
