# ADHD High Performance Habits: Moving From Forced Discipline To Intuitive Sprints And Recovery

High-performing individuals with ADHD do not fight their biological nature; they architect their habits around it. The shift from "forced discipline" to "intuitive sprints" allows for massive output without the shame-cycle of traditional productivity.

- **Rest Choice:** Low performers feel rest is wasted time; high performers take rest on purpose to avoid burnout-forced recovery.
- **Checklist Menu:** Low performers treat checklists as strict orders; high performers treat them like menus, choosing what fits their current energy.
- **Emotional Reset:** Low performers carry the guilt of unfinished tasks; high performers acknowledge emotions in the morning and start with a clean slate.

## The Why
