# The Post-Launch SEO Checklist: 5 Mandatory Steps To Validate Your Website's Existence

Search engine optimization is not magic; it is a technical checklist. If a website has zero visitors after launch, it is usually because the developer skipped the foundational steps required to announce the site's existence to Google and Bing.

## Breakdown
1. **Google Search Console:** The primary way to tell Google your site exists. Free and takes 5 minutes.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools:** 10% of global search is ignored by competitors. Capture this traffic for free.
3. **Sitemap Submission:** Feed search engines a map of all your pages to ensure proper crawling.
4. **IndexNow:** Ping search engines instantly when you publish new content for near-real-time indexing.
5. **Open Graph Tags:** Configure og:title, og:image, and og:description to ensure links look professional when shared on social platforms.

## The Why
Visibility is a technical requirement. By moving from \"Hoping Google finds it\" to \"Follow the checklist,\" you remove the unpredictability of organic traffic, turning launch-day silence into a technical problem that has been solved.
