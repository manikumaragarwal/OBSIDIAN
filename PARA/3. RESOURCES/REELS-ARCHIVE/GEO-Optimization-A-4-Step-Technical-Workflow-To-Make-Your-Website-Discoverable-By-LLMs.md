# GEO Optimization: A 4-Step Technical Workflow To Make Your Website Discoverable By LLMs

Traditional SEO is evolving into GEO (Generative Engine Optimization). To ensure ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity recommend your website, you must feed them structured data (JSON) based on the specific questions your audience is asking AI engines.

## Breakdown
1. **Query Sourcing:** Use **Perplexity.ai** to identify the frequently asked questions (FAQs) related to your specific business niche.
2. **JSON Generation:** Feed these FAQs into ChatGPT or Claude to generate a structured JSON-LD data set specifically formatted for LLM ingestion.
3. **Validation:** Use Google's **Rich Results Test** (code mode) to verify that the generated JSON is technically valid.
4. **Injection:** Paste the validated JSON into your website's header to ensure multiple LLMs can "recognize" and recommend your site.

## Tool Stack
- **Perplexity:** For data sourcing.
- **ChatGPT / Claude:** For structured data generation.
- **Google Rich Results Test:** For technical validation.

## The Why
AI engines don't "crawl" like Google; they "ingest" structured knowledge. By providing an LLM-friendly FAQ layer in JSON format, you are moving to the front of the line for generative answers, ensuring your site is the one the AI chooses to cite.
