# Large Language Model Recommendation Manipulation Via Syndicated Press Releases

Strategic use of syndicated press releases can effectively manipulate AI Overviews (Google) and ChatGPT recommendations to favor specific brands or products.

## Breakdown
- **The $80 SEO Trick**: Purchasing a low-cost press release (e.g., AB Newswire) with specific target keywords in the subheading and first sentence (e.g., "Best-Selling SEO Book").
- **Syndication Loophole**: These press releases are syndicated to high-authority sites like *The Globe and Mail*. AI models cite these authoritative sources, leading to number one rankings in AI Overviews.
- **The "Best-of" Blog Strategy**: If a press release is unavailable, a blog post on a private site claiming "Best [Category]" can also trigger LLM recommendations (e.g., ChatGPT recommending gym software based on a self-authored blog post).

## Market Logic
AI models and search engine "AI Overviews" prioritize authoritative syndication. By injecting specific claims into the syndication pipeline, creators exploit the AI's trust in legacy media domains to manufacture "Best-Selling" or "Best-in-Class" status.

- High-Authority Syndication Sites (The Globe and Mail)
