# Curiosity Mapping: A Reflective Journaling Framework For Developing Your Independent Perspective

Curiosity is a muscle that must be exercised through non-judgmental reflection. "Curiosity Mapping" is a journaling framework that decenters the human experience by forcing an individual to analyze their "What and Why" across general interests, revealing the deep-seated identity beneath social perceptions.

## Breakdown
1. **Interest Inventory:** List your general interests (e.g., animal behavior, history, tech). Crucial: Do NOT judge them by relevance or social coolness.
2. **Stage One (Childhood Reflection):** If stuck, reflect on what fascinated you as a child in elementary school.
3. **The Push (What & Why):** For a single interest, ask:
   - **What** specific behaviors or sub-topics fascinate me? (e.g., Orcas' matriarchal structure).
   - **Why** does this trigger my curiosity? (e.g., It decenters humans as the sole source of emotion).
4. **Intentional Observation:** Use the map to notice where your eyes go in the real world and on social media, turning passive scrolling into intentional data collection.

## The Why
We often only explore things that make us look "important." Curiosity mapping removes the ego, allowing you to discover the "Existing Data" in your own mind. This is the foundation of original thought—becoming intentional about the questions you ask the world based on your innate fascinations.
