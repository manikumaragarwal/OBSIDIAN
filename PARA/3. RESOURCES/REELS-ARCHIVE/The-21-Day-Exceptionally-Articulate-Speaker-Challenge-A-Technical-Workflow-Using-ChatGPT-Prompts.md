# The 21-Day Exceptionally Articulate Speaker Challenge: A Technical Workflow Using ChatGPT Prompts

Articulate speaking is a result of high-volume, low-friction practice. By using ChatGPT to generate tiered impromptu prompts and following a strict 21-day recording schedule, any speaker can eliminate filler words and maintain composure under pressure.

## Breakdown
1. **Prompt Generation:** Ask ChatGPT for 100 impromptu prompts ranging from easy to hard, answerable in 2 minutes.
2. **Days 1-7 (Foundation):** 3 easy prompts daily. 1-minute speeches. Focus on no filler words (pause instead).
3. **Days 8-14 (Structure):** Medium prompts. Focus on flow and recovery—if you make a mistake, don't restart.
4. **Days 15-21 (Pressure):** Hard prompts. Extended 1.5-minute timer. Practice composure under difficult technical subjects.

## Tool Stack
- **ChatGPT:** For prompt generation.
- **Voice Recorder:** For daily takes and self-review.

## The Why
Filler words are a symptom of the brain moving faster than the tongue. The "Pause over Filler" habit, practiced over 21 days, rewires the nervous system to handle the silence of thought as a signal of authority rather than a sign of failure.
