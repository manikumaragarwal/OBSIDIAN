# The Visual Copy Framework: Why Design Exists To Make Copy Understood

Design is not decoration; it is an aid for comprehension. The fundamental reason for high-end UI is to ensure that the core sales copy "lands" in the user's mind with zero resistance, moving from passive scrolling to active understanding through visual hierarchy.

## Breakdown
- **The 80/20 Rule:** 80% of conversion happens through the copy (the logic); 20% happens through the design (the delivery).
- **Representation Gap:** Large blocks of text create a comprehension barrier. Design's job is to bridge this gap through visual aids.
- **The Goal of UI:** Move the user from "I see words" to "I understand the value proposition" through animations and components that highlight the copy's hierarchy.

## The Why
Poor representation is the silent killer of good copywriting. By recognizing that design exists solely to facilitate the understanding of the copy, a developer can move from "making it pretty" to "making it clear," which is the true driver of high-conversion landing pages.
