# REBUILDING A STORYTELLING ACCOUNT FROM SCRATCH: 4 KEY STEPS

To rebuild a storytelling account from scratch, focus on creating a mantra, studying other creators, learning scriptwriting, and improving incrementally.

## Breakdown
1. Write a mantra that all videos point back to, driving towards the same message,
2. Study other creators who are good at storytelling, follow them, and take notes,
3. Learn what makes a good script, focusing on voice, format, and replication,
4. Improve incrementally, focusing on: talking to the camera, engaging captions, using music for emotional delivery, and sound design

## The Why
Having a clear mantra and studying other creators helps to establish a consistent message and voice, while incremental improvement allows for refinement and growth.
