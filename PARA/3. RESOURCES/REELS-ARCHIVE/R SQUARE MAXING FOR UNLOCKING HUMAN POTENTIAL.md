# R SQUARE MAXING FOR UNLOCKING HUMAN POTENTIAL

## The Mathematics of Truthful Performance
The coefficient of determination ($R^2$) doesn't just explain data—it explains people. Think of $R^2$ as: 
`1 - (Interpretation of Performance / Actual Truthful Performance)`
We all start out at a 1 (our full, unhindered potential). But the moment we begin to obsess over the narrative, second-guess ourselves, and rewrite the story in our heads, we start to chip away at everything we can actually fully become.

## The "Aha!" Moment and Paradigm Shift
The "Aha!" moment comes from looking at Oprah: She was fired from her first TV job for being "unfit for television." However, her interpretation of her performance was never the verdict they gave her. She refused to internalize it. She kept shrinking the gap between how she saw herself and what she was actually producing. 

**The long-term shift:** Stop performing for the narrative and start performing for the result. By reducing our psychological dependence on what we think we are doing, and putting that exact energy into the actual work, we can hit our craziest goals in a quarter of the time.
