# Curating The Curators: A 4-Step Workflow To Prevent Recommendation Fatigue And Develop Original Taste

In an era where everyone is a curator, the danger is "passive consumption" where saving a post gives a fake dopamine rush of learning. To develop original taste, you must move from saving to reflecting through a disciplined curation workflow.

## Breakdown
1. **Centralize:** Create a single folder in your notes app for links from all platforms (Substack, YouTube, Twitter, etc.) to prevent "platform silos."
2. **Selection:** Stop exploring everything. Identify the few creators whose taste you truly trust and prioritize their recommendations.
3. **Dedicated Review:** Set a specific time to consume. Rule: Watch/read one saved recommendation *before* consuming anything new.
4. **Reflect:** Take notes and look inward. Curation is the bridge to developing your own independent taste instead of borrowing it.

## Tool Stack
- **Notes App:** Centralized link database.
- **Time Blocking:** For dedicated review sessions.

## The Why
Passive saving is a dopamine trap. By forcing a review phase and taking notes, you move information from "temporary data" to "permanent knowledge," which is the only way to build a confident and original perspective in a crowded digital space.
