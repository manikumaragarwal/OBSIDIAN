# Tension Stacking: The Concept Of Multi-Loop Retention

Retention in video content lies in the strategic use of tension; by stacking multiple open loops throughout a script, creators can psychologically incentivize viewers to watch until the end.

## Breakdown
- **Open vs. Closed Loops**: Most creators use open loops in their hooks but close them too early, satisfying the viewer's curiosity and causing them to scroll away.
- **The Tension Loop Concept**: Instead of a single hook, "re-hook" the viewer repeatedly throughout the script using tension loops.
- **Loop Stacking**: Stack 2-3 loops throughout the video. This creates a psychological "swindle" where the viewer feels compelled to finish the video to resolve the tension.
- **The Payoff Requirement**: An unanswered loop feels like wasted time. To maintain trust and authority, every stacked loop must have a satisfying "Answer" at the end.

## The Why
The framework relies on the idea that "Retention lies in Tension." By moving from a single-hook structure to a multi-loop tension stack, creators move from "Hoping for views" to "Engineered Watch Time."

## Script Spark
> "If you satisfy your viewer's curiosity in the first 10 seconds, you've just given them a reason to scroll; stack your loops so they can't leave until the end."
