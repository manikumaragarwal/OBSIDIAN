# 21-DAY CHALLENGE TO BECOME AN EXCEPTIONALLY ARTICULATE SPEAKER

Spend 21 days practicing impromptu speeches using ChatGPT prompts to improve communication skills and become an articulate speaker.

## Breakdown
- Days 1-7: Answer easy prompts, set a timer for 1 minute, and record one take daily.
- Days 8-14: Answer medium prompts, maintain structure and timing, and focus on flow and recovery.,
- Days 15-21: Answer hard prompts, extend timer to 1 minute 30 seconds, and practice maintaining composure and structure under pressure.

## The Why
This challenge helps build confidence and discipline in public speaking by gradually increasing the difficulty level of prompts and encouraging participants to maintain structure, timing, and composure.
