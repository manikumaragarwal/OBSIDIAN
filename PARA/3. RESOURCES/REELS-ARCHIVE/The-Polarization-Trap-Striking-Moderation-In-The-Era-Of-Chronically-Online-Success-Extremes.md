# The Polarization Trap: Striking Moderation In The Era Of Chronically Online Success Extremes

Social media algorithms optimize for viral extremes, pushing users toward unhealthy obsessions with money or performative moral uprightness. Finding true fulfillment requires recognizing that viral \"success frameworks\" (like Hormozi's) are often distorted for engagement.

## Breakdown
- **The Addiction Cycle:** Starting with a goal (e.g., 100k/month) but ending with a loss of empathy, peace, and presence.
- **The False Binary:** The internet pushes you to either \"Obsess over money\" or \"Say it's not about the money.\" Both extremes lead to failure (burnout or unprofitability).
- **The Hormozi Paradox:** Viral content tells you to skip family Christmas; the next video calls that demonic. Neither is entirely true.
- **The Solution:** Stop being chronically online. The truth exists in the moderation that algorithms cannot monetize.

## The Why
Identity is fragile under the pressure of constant consumption. By identifying the \"Polarization Trap,\" a creator can build a profitable business without sacrificing their humanity to the viral extremes of success culture.
