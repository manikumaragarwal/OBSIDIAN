# The SEO Blind Spot Of Vibecoding: Why React-Based AI Apps Struggle With Google Indexing

AI-built apps often suffer from a hidden SEO liability: client-side rendering. Websites generated by tools like Lovable or Replit often use pure React code, which presents an "empty page" to Google's crawlers, causing indexing delays of weeks and burning through crawl budgets.

## Breakdown
- **Client-Side Rendering (CSR):** Google's crawlers see an empty page first and must return later to render it, putting the site at a massive disadvantage against plain HTML.
- **Crawl Budget:** Google only spends a limited time on any site; CSR sites burn this budget just trying to reveal their basic content.
- **The Fix (Next.js):** Moving to Next.js allows for server-side rendering, ensuring Google sees the full content immediately with no delay.
- **AI Instructions:** When using AI builders, explicitly ask for plain HTML/CSS or a migration to Next.js to avoid the React indexing trap.

## The Why
Visibility is as important as functionality. A high-quality AI app that cannot be found by Google is a business failure. Understanding the "Blind Spot" of client-side rendering allows founders to move from "building for users" to "building for discovery."
