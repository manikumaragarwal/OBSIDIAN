`# The Original Thought Muscle: How To Reclaim Your Independent Perspective In A Frictionless Economy

Originality is not an innate trait; it is a muscle that must be practiced by introducing friction into the consumption loop. In an environment designed for immediate sharing and borrowed opinions, the ability to sit with an idea before seeking external validation is the only way to develop a unique perspective.

## Breakdown
- **The Friction Deficit:** Information is now shared before it is understood. This lack of friction causes "mass-opinion" where everyone adopts the same borrowed views.
- **The Review Trap:** We often run to the comment section or YouTube reviews to decide what we think about a movie or book, rather than capturing our raw, unfiltered reaction.
- **Half-Formed Thoughts:** The first step to independent thinking is being willing to state an initial disagreement, even before you can articulate the "why."
- **Sitting in Stillness:** You must sit quietly long enough to notice your own thoughts coming up before we let the internet answer them for you.

## The Why
Modern consumption is designed to outsource our feedback to the loudest voices in the room. By intentionally delaying the "Review Phase" and sitting with the raw information, you find out who you are underneath the constant noise of borrowed opinions. Complexity and originality are found in the time spent between consumption and reaction.
