# Paneer Chemistry: The Secret Technique For Transforming Rubbery Paneer Into Silky Sauce

The assumption that "Paneer doesn't melt" is a chemical misconception. Paneer is structurally bound by calcium, which causes it to become rubbery under heat. By using a small timing and mixing trick with sodium bicarbonate (baking soda) and citric acid (lemon), you can relax the proteins and turn paneer into a silky, glossy sauce.

## Breakdown
1. **The Problem:** Calcium binds the paneer proteins too tightly, preventing melting and leading to a rubbery texture.
2. **The Relaxer:** Add a combination of **Calcium Sequestering agents** (like baking soda) and a touch of lemon juice to the paneer.
3. **The Chemical Stop:** Once the fizzing stops, the proteins have relaxed, and the grainy texture vanishes.
4. **Outcome:** A silky liquid paneer base that does not separate or taste sour.
