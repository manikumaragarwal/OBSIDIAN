# Classic Fiction For Intellectual Growth: 5 Books To Unrot Your Brain

The fastest path to an "intellectual glow-up" is engaging with high-resistance classic fiction. These 5 novels force the reader to navigate existentialism, systemic absurdity, and complex human impulses.

## Breakdown
- **The Meek One (Dostoevsky):** An underrated study of a narcissistic, abusive man who drives his wife to tragedy.
- **Nausea (Jean-Paul Sartre):** A gateway into French existentialism and the search for inherent meaning in life.
- **The Trial (Franz Kafka):** Follows a protagonist accused of an unknown crime—a masterclass in the feeling of powerlessness in the absurd.
- **The Queen of Spades (Pushkin):** A cautionary tale where a man's obsession with a card game's secret leads to his downfall.
- **The Brothers Karamazov (Dostoevsky):** A life-changing philosophical novel that explores the fundamental nature of man.

## The Why
