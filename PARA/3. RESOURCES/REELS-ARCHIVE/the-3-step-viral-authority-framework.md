# The 3-Step Viral Authority Framework

A tactical breakdown for creating high-retention videos that convert views into high-ticket DMs from 7 and 8-figure founders.

## Breakdown
1. **The Authority Hook**:
    - State your identity (e.g., age/position) and tie it to a bold claim with a clear dream outcome.
    - Use a fast-paced sequence of clips to stop the scroll.
    - Create an "Open Loop" by promising a specific number of steps to achieve the outcome.
2. **The High-Value Body**:
    - Fulfill the promise made in the hook by providing "Max Value."
    - Explain the "What" and "Why," while explicitly calling out common mistakes.
    - Use real-time visuals and personal decision-making examples to build credibility and trust.
3. **The Targeted CTA**:
    - Directly call out your ideal client (e.g., entrepreneurs, founders).
    - Repeat the dream outcome from the hook.
    - Provide a clear next step (e.g., "DM me the word BRAND") tied to the video's value.

## Tool Stack
- Fast-paced clip sequences (B-roll)
- On-screen visuals (Showing process in real-time)
- DM Automation/Keyword Triggers (e.g., ManyChat)

## The Why
The framework relies on "Authority Positioning" rather than just "Attention Grabbing." By combining a bold claim with transparent, visual proof of the process, the creator moves from being a generic "tip-giver" to a credible expert that high-level founders want to hire.
