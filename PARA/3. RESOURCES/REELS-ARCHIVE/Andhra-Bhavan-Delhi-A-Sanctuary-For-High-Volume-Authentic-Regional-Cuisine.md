# Andhra Bhavan Delhi: A Sanctuary For High-Volume, Authentic Regional Cuisine

Delhi's state bhavans function as cultural and culinary gateways. Andhra Bhavan is recognized as the primary spot for authentic, high-volume South Indian regional cuisine, offering a "humbled canteen" environment that prioritizes food quality over plating aesthetics.
