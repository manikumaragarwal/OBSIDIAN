# JAMES CK LIM ON ENGINEERING YOUR PERFORMER ENVIRONMENT

## The Directionless Racehorse
If you are someone who can always work exceptionally hard for others but cannot seem to make any progress on your own personal projects or business, you are not lazy. You are like a powerful racehorse that can perform on the track but is completely directionless in an open field. The root of the issue is environmental: you simply haven't created the right structure to pull out the "performer" side of yourself.

## The Strategy
James notes that the ultimate trick to fixing this is diagnostic engineering. You must figure out the exact conditions that bring out your "performer" at their best—deep down, you already know what these conditions are—and consciously engineer those same constraints, pressures, or setups so they work for your personal endeavors.
