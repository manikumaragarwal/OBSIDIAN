# The Viral Video Checklist: 5 Tactical Rules For Breaking The Instagram Algorithm

Virality is a technical result of aligning your content with platform-native consumption patterns. By using a data-backed checklist—from hook mirroring to emotional music selection—a creator can consistently hit millions of views by working *with* the algorithm rather than against it.

## Breakdown
1. **Hook Mirroring:** Search TikTok for niche keywords. Only watch videos with 50k+ likes. Mirror their hooks word-for-word in your own content.
2. **The Plastic Font:** Always use the standard TikTok/Instagram fonts. The brain is programmed to consume what is comfortable and familiar.
3. **Zero Dead Space:** Cut every single breath or pause. In short-form, speed is the only way to prevent the skip.
4. **Instrumental Emotion:** Use music that matches the video's emotion but has **zero words**. Do not compete with the background song for the viewer's attention.
5. **Format Alignment:** Identify what is already working in your niche and double down on that specific technical style.

## The Why
The human brain seeks the "Path of Least Resistance." By using familiar fonts and mirroring proven hooks, you are lowering the cognitive barrier for the viewer to consume your information. You aren't fighting the tide; you are surfing what the data proves is already working.
