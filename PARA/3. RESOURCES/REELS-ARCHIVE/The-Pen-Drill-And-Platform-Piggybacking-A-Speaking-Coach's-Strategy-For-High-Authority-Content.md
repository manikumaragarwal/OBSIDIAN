# The Pen Drill And Platform Piggybacking: A Speaking Coach's Strategy For High-Authority Content

Authority is built by demonstrating expertise on already trending platforms. By using a viral creator (like Kai Cenat) as a case study, you piggyback on their reach while providing high-value technical drills that establish you as the silent expert in the room.

## Breakdown
1. **The Platform Piggyback:** Identify a viral video or creator and use their existing platform to share your specific knowledge.
2. **The Pen Drill:** Read out loud with a pen held horizontally in your mouth. This forces the tongue and jaw to work harder, leading to instant clarity in enunciation once the pen is removed.
3. **Obnoxious Enunciation:** Practice reading drills where even minor words like "of" or "thus" are enunciated to an exaggerated extent to combat fast-talking.
4. **The CTA Integration:** Use a waitlist or early access offer (e.g., Speakeasy) as a logical next step for those who tried the drill and saw results.

You aren't just telling them you're an expert; you are proving it through their own voice.
