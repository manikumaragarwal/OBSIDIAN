# The Concert Theory: Attention As Leverage

In a stadium of 25,000 people, you have only five seconds to create an emotional reaction before the crowd leaves. Content creation is the digital equivalent of this high-stakes environment.

## Breakdown
- **The 5-Second Rule**: Cleverness is secondary to grasping attention. If the first sentence doesn't create an immediate "lean-in" moment, the viewer is lost.
- **Tension Over Context**: Traditional openings often provide too much context. Effective hooks start with "Tension"—making the viewer feel seen, challenged, or curious.
- **The "Lean-In" Test**: Every post should be evaluated by a single question: "If I said this on a stage, would people lean in or go back to their phones?"

## The Why
This shift moves content from "Information Delivery" to "Attention Grabbing." In the attention economy, attention is the primary leverage; without it, the most valuable information remains invisible. The "Aha!" is that talking *more* is the enemy of saying the *right* thing *first*.

## Script Spark
