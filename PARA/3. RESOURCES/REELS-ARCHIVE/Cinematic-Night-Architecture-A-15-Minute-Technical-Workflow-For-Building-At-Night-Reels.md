# Cinematic Night Architecture: A 15-Minute Technical Workflow For Building-At-Night Reels

Capturing the "Vibe" of night-time architecture is an editing sequence, not just a filming one. By splitting clips into a 3-part graphic-and-mask sequence and aligning them with a specific audio tempo, editors can create high-gloss architecture reels in under 15 minutes.

## Breakdown
1. **Clip Selection:** Source 3-5 clips of illuminated buildings at night (skyscrapers, bridges, monuments).
2. **Audio Alignment:** Match the clips to the tempo of a chosen song.
3. **The 3-Part Split:** Split each clip into three segments:
   - **Part 1 (Graphic):** Max out graphic adjusters for high contrast.
   - **Part 2 (Mask):** Apply a corner mask effect for visual intrigue.
   - **Part 3 (Regular):** The clean, un-edited clip for the resolution.
4. **Final Polish:** Add text overlays and export.

## Tool Stack
- **Final Cut Pro:** For the graphic and corner mask effects.
- **iPhone 17 Pro Max:** (Recommended hardware for high-dynamic-range night shots).

## The Why
Night visuals are high-retention by nature due to the high contrast and mood. By following a technical 15-minute workflow, creators can produce cinematic-quality visual assets that signal a high production value without the need for traditional cinema cameras or hours of manual grading.
