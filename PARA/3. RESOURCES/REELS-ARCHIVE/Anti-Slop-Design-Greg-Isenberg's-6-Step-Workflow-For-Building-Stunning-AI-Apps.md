# Anti-Slop Design: Greg Isenberg's 6-Step Workflow For Building Stunning AI Apps

Most vibe-coded apps fail because they look like "AI Slop." To build visually stunning products, a developer must separate the "Logic Phase" from the "Soul Phase," using AI to refine aesthetics only after the core functionality is locked in.

## Breakdown
1. **Ugly Prototype:** Build the base version in Google AI Studio with zero aesthetic prompts. Focus on logic and functionality first.
2. **Define the Soul:** Once logic is set, prompt Claude for a specific mood (e.g., "calm and analog") to avoid generic text-heavy UI.
3. **Taste Sourcing:** Pick 5-10 reference images from Pinterest-alternatives and feed them to AI builders to anchor your taste.
4. **Cohesive Palette:** Use **Flux2 Pro** to build a visual anchor (e.g., a vintage cassette) that guides the app's color and texture.
5. **Logo Engineering:** Use **Ideogram V3** with strong negative prompts to generate a professional, non-generic logo.
6. **Vibe Assembly:** Assemble everything in Figma and feed the screenshots back into the AI builder to code toward the "North Star" design.

## Tool Stack
- **Google AI Studio:** For logic prototyping.
- **Claude:** For brand-soul definition.
- **Flux2 Pro:** For palette anchoring.
- **Ideogram V3:** For professional logos.
- **Figma:** For final assembly.

## The Why
Design is the signal of trust. By following this 6-step workflow, you are moving from "AI-generated slop" to intentional design engineering. You use AI as your designer's hands, but you maintain the designer's brain by sourcing your own taste and anchoring the brand in a specific "Soul."
