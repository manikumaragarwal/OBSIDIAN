# Sean Werbowskii: Modeling Successful Outlier Content For Accelerated Growth

## Overview
This workflow breaks the traditional rules of content strategy by advocating for "stealing like an artist." Instead of focusing on original creation from scratch, the process relies on identifying and deconstructing outlier content that is already performing at 2-3x the average engagement in a specific niche. This method allowed a brand new account to hit 600,000 views and close a $1,200 inbound deal in just 17 days.

## The Workflow / Tutorial

1. **Niche Definition:** Get extremely clear on your specific niche or adjacent niches.
2. **Outlier Identification:** 
    - Use the Edits App inspiration tab to find small accounts (5k–50k followers).
    - Look for posts from the last 3–4 months.
    - Identify "outlier content"—videos outperforming their average engagement by a 2x-3x multiple.
3. **Deconstruction:** Take apart the outlier video piece by piece:
    - Analyze the **Hook Structure**.
    - Examine the **Caption** and **Visuals**.
    - Identify the underlying **Story** or **Transformation**.
4. **Unique Application:** Apply the exact same structure to your own unique video ideas, niche, and content.
5. **Execution Volume:** Execute this process 3–5 times a day.

## Tool Stack
- **Edits App (Inspiration Tab):** Used for finding successful small accounts and current trends.
- **Instagram Reels:** The primary platform for identifying outlier engagement.

## Strategic Logic
The "success leaves clues" philosophy suggests that modeling what is already working is the fastest path to growth. By focusing on small accounts (5k-50k), you identify strategies that are effective without the "authority bias" of massive followings. The $1,200 inbound deal demonstrates that high-retention modeling leads directly to high-intent leads, even on new accounts.
