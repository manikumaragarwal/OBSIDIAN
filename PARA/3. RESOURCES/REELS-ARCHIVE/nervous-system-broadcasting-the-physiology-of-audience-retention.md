# Nervous System Broadcasting: The Physiology Of Audience Retention

Your physical and emotional state on camera is subconsciously mirrored by your audience; tension in the creator leads to friction in the viewer.

## Breakdown
- **Emotional Mirroring**: Humans are wired to unconsciously mirror the emotional state of those they watch. If you are tense, rushed, or second-guessing yourself, the viewer feels an "offness" and scrolls away.
- **The "Faking Confidence" Myth**: Faking confidence is still a form of performance that creates subtle tension.
- **Pre-Record Regulation**: Effective presence starts with physical regulation before hitting record—slow breaths, dropping the shoulders, and clearing cortisol.

## The Why
This shift moves presence from "Acting" to "Regulating." The "Aha!" moment is that the quality of your message is secondary to the quality of your physical state; if the audience mirrors your tension, they will associate your message with discomfort.
