# THE TRUE VALUE OF PERFORMATIVE ACTIVISM

## Is the Performance the Point?
Society has turned the word "performative" into a slur—a reason to dismiss and ultimately a reason to do nothing. While not all performance is equal (e.g., a corporation posting a rainbow logo in June while simultaneously donating to anti-LGBTQ+ causes is genuinely hollow), there is a massive and meaningful difference between performance that costs nothing and achieves nothing, versus performance that produces *some* movement in the world, even if the motive is imperfect.

## The "Aha!" Moment
The defining realization is that essentially everything we do publicly is performative to some degree. The shift in thinking requires us to stop asking, "Is this performative?" and instead ask functional questions: 
- What does it do? 
- Who does it reach? 
- What does it cost the person doing it? 
- Does it generate attention, money, solidarity, or tangible change? 
- Or does it exist purely for the comfort of the performer?
