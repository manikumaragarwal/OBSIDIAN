# The Traffic Foundation: 5 Essential SEO Steps To Validate Your Website Before Running Ads

Running ads on an un-optimized website is a waste of capital. Before spending on traffic, a business must ensure the "Traffic Foundation" is set through five critical technical SEO steps that explain the website's existence to Google.

## Breakdown
1. **Search Console:** Claim your property to see how Google views your site.
2. **Sitemap:** Submit a clear XML sitemap to ensure all pages are indexable.
3. **Google Business Listing:** Optimize your local listing for regional visibility.
4. **Open Graph Tags:** Ensure your site looks professional when shared on social platforms.
5. **IndexNow:** Use the IndexNow protocol to notify engines of content updates instantly.

## The Why
Website creation is only half the job; the other half is explaining the site to search engines. If you ignore free traffic foundations, you will be forced to overpay for ads just to maintain visibility. A validated foundation is the only way to build long-term, sustainable traffic.
