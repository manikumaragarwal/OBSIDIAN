# The 9 Non-Negotiable Rules For Life Agency And Philosophical Success

- **Intellectual Agency:** Learn how to self-educate; develop a high-value skill; develop a personal philosophy on success.
- **Social Integrity:** Keep all promises to yourself and others; take romantic relationships seriously.
- **Spiritual Discipline:** Meditate; take accountability and agency in your life.
