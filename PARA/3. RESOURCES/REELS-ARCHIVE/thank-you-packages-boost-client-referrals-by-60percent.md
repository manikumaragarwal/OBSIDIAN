# THANK YOU PACKAGES BOOST CLIENT REFERRALS BY 60%

Sending a thoughtful thank you package to clients after project completion can lead to a significant increase in referrals and repeat business. By including a Notion doc, Loom video, and a simple referral request, you can make clients feel taken care of and more likely to refer others.

## Breakdown
1. Create a Notion doc with client website passwords, brand colors, and self-update instructions (10-minute effort),
2. Record a 3-minute Loom video walking the client through their new site,
3. Send a simple message asking if they know anyone who needs a website (non-pushy referral request)

## The Why
By going above and beyond in the delivery process, you can create a positive experience that fosters loyalty and encourages clients to refer others. This approach shifts the focus from mere project completion to building a long-term relationship with the client.
