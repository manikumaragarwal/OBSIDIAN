# Define your Brand

**Branding** - pairing of things
**Good Branding** - intentional pairing of relevant things, done consistently



**Brand Journey Framework**
1. What do I want to have happen?
2. What do I have to be known for that to happen?
3. What do I have to do in order to be known for that?
4. What do I have to learn in order to do those things?

> *If you do not have a desired outcome that you're making your decision you're off of... but it might not be the place you were hoping for. *

# Position your Brand


**List your pivotal moment in your story, challenges you've overcome.**




People are constantly thinking about objection to what you're trying to say.
- See your comments, see what the people are trying to comment, and start addressing those issues in your new video.

- **Share not only your wins, but your losses**. 
	- people relates with you more, when you share your failures (exactly how [[Aristotle's Poetics|Aristotle]] talks about [[Catharsis|audience feeling pity for the tragic hero, they relates with him]]
	- Sharing your Losses is better than sharing your Wins, it instantly connects your audience with yourself.

> [!NOTE] Aristotelian Catharsis in Content Creation
> This is a direct application of [[Aristotle's Poetics|Aristotle's catharsis]] to modern personal branding:
> - Aristotle: audiences feel *pity* for the tragic hero → emotional connection
> - Branding theory: sharing *your failures* = same emotional resonance
> - The hero's vulnerability (hamartia, fatal flaw) is what makes them relatable — not their victories
> See [[3 Tragic Beats]] for the tragic hero framework, and how [[Doctor Faustus - Christopher Marlowe|Faustus]] or [[Mahabharata|Karna]] become compelling precisely because of their flaws.

> *Don't follow a trend just because it's a trend. Stay true to yourself*. 


# BRAND STORY FRAMEWORK
A strong brand **stands out**.

**Questions to determine my core Truth :**
1. What do I believe that others do not. 
2. What is in my personality that stands out?
3. What do people compliment me on, that I can lean into/ double down on?

- These are my **Core Truths**, this is how I can reinforce my Identity. 

- My Brand is not what I say, but what I do and prove, over and over again.
- Strong brands have a pattern of ongoing credibility, instead of just one past success story. 

> [!TIP] "Core Truths" across domains
> - In [[OUTWORK everyone by being BORED|Boredom & Productivity theory]]: being truly creative requires silence and self-honesty — the same self-awareness needed to identify your "Core Truths"
> - In [[Am I a fake person]]: "I lied to avoid humiliation" — knowing your honest motivations (not the sanitized ones) is the foundation of authentic branding
> - [[Thoughts I had while watching Bojack Horseman]] explores the gap between who we are and who we project — branding forces a confrontation with that gap

# Picking your Topic
If I start talking about everything in the beginning, you would dilute the power of what I am speaking. 

You should **expand your topic** to adjacent topics, and not completely New Topics.
