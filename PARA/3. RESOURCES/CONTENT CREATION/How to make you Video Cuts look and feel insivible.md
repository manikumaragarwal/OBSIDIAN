# How to make you Video Cuts look and feel insivible
1. Add **B-rolls**, motion graphics, overlays, on top of cuts, to hide the cuts..  
2. Cut on **Music Beat**.   (add a [[How to use Sound Fx for your Instagram Reels|woosh]], or riser on top on the edit, to make it more seamless)
3. Cut on **Motion**, record you scripts in such a way, so that your 2 consecutive sentences end and start in middle of a similiar motion.
4. The **J - cut**, start your audio a little early
		XXXXXXXX|YYYYYYYY
		VVVVVV|AAAAAAAAAA
--- [PROFRESHIONAL SKILLS]
5. make a slight **zoom-in** on the clip (after the cut).
6. Make sure your cuts **matches the eyes location**... (dono clips before and after cutting, ki eyes same place par ho!!)
7. Instead of shooting the whole video on one angle, shoot the same script twice, on **2 different angles**... and then edit those clips, to make it cohesive... (changing the angle on cut, will make it look more organic.)
	*you can even colorgrade your scripts, so that you have different sentences to shoot on different angles*
