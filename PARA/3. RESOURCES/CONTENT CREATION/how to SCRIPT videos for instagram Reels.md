# how to script videos for instagram Reels


##  Stage 1: RELATABLE PAIN HOOK - 1 sentence

**GOAL**:
Stop scroll + Open an emotional loop. Make the viewer feel "wait, this is about me".

**RULES**: 
- First 1.5 seconds (**15 words or less**)
- Use a universal belief, fear, or shame through. Try to use "you".

**HOOK TEMPLATE**:
- I don't know ==who== needs to hear this, but...
- "==You== ever feel like...."
- "Here's why ==you== still(bad thing), even when you're doing everything right."
- "This mindset will not make ==you== successful"

> [!CONNECTION] Use "**You**" so that the audience feel heard
> We have learned this in [[Aristotle's Poetics]] as well, that the purpose of any piece of drama is to [[Catharsis|invoke emotions in the audience heart]].. 
> This is important, because this makes the audience feel connected to the story, as if the story is about them... they see a part of themself in our story. 


## Stage 2: BACKSTORY / SETUP - 2-4 short sentence
****
**PURPOSE**:
Make story feel personal, real and ==vulnerable==, Creates the "*literally me*" connection.

**RULES**:
- No sentence longer than 12 words (story progresses quicker, more early dopamine)
- Inject specific language (locations, age, exact numbers) "I used to..." -> "==2 years ago.=="

**TEMPLATES**:
- "==2 years ago==, I was in ==college==, posting random videos from my ==dorm==, posting random videos from my ==dorm==."
- "I had ==0== followers, no strategy, just posting random ==Among Us memes==."
- "I told myself ==every day== for ==4 months== straight that ==I'd start tomorrow=="
## Stage 3: BREAKING POINT - 1-2 sentence
**PURPOSE**:
Reveal the *moment* everything changed.

**RULES**:
- Describe your transformation in one ==MOMENT==, not a gradual process.
- "I started to realize" -> **"I saw this reel that said..."**
- Use contrast words : ==**but, untill, then I realized**==

**Templates**:
- "But nothing ever changed."
- "It finally hit me: grinding harder wasn't the answer."
- "It wasn't lazy. I was burnt out."
## Stage 4:TAKEAWAY - 2-3 sentence

**PURPOSE**:
Delivers the real, unexpected truth. Feels earned not preachy, after the story.

**RULES**:
- Emotional honesty turns into ==practical== wisdom
- Keep tone calm and conversational (*not preachy*)
- 1 sentence of what resulted *AFTER* you applied this wisdom. (==this is the "payoff"==)

**TEMPLATES**: 
- "The truth is, shame doesn't make you more disciplined. It just makes you hate the process."
- "Once I stopped trying to be perfect, I started making actual progress. I started hitting gym every day consistently, instead of once a week."
- "I realized I didnt' need more time. I needed to forgive myself."
- "Work doesn't feel hard anymore because I like who I am when I'm working."
## Stage 5: SOFT CTA/ BUTTON (optional) - 1 sentence

**PURPOSE**:
Invite next step without sounding desperate or pitchy.

**RULES**:
Make the CTA build on the previous sentence, using "But, And, So"

**TEMPLATE**:
- "So, comment 'monk mode' if this is where you're at."
- "And save this for a day you forget your why."
- "But I'm breaking this down deeper in my class this week."
- "Aight? I'll see yall tomorrow."

## Other weird shit I personally do that make my content sound "authentic" lol.

**USE THESE TONES**:
- **"Best friend rant" tone: raw, slightly messy, no fake polish**
	- Pretend your friend is LITERALLY in front of you.
- "**Let me pull back the curtain for you**" = creator insider tips
	- Advvice in the framing of "I discovered this hack ," as opposed to "==i'm a genius, you suck!=="
- "**Self-roast tone**": honest, slightly embarrassed past self
	- See slang phrase below. Don't take yourself too seriously.
**Slang Phrase Mino naturally uses that garners *empathy + friendly tone***
- "Pause."
- "Little stupid shit like..."
- "I know this sounds cringe but..."
- "Bro, that shit is so sad."
- "I'm not shitting on anyone, just saying.."
- "I thought I had to grind 24/7 but..."

**Short Sentences, in repetition**
- Say a short phrase 2-3x for rhythm: 
  "You scroll, You watch. You procrastinate. And now you feel like shit."
- Mirror your own thought loops: 
  "I didn't post. So my views taked. So I didn't want to post again."

Use the **[[Three Act Structure|Three ACT Structure]] used in Film** for scripting your Reels : 
- ACT ONE : set up
- ACT TWO : confrontation
- ACT THREE :resolution
