**Body**

I don’t know if this is ADHD or just me…

but I start things with insane energy.

New study plan.  
New routine.  
New project.  
New phase of life.

I go all in.

Last year I was preparing for NEET.

Every day I was in the library from morning till night.

Big jar of black coffee.  
Terrible food.  
No real sleep.

I wasn’t lazy.

That’s the part that confuses me.

I was trying.

Really trying.

But somewhere in the middle…

it stopped feeling like progress.

My friends were already in college.

Moving on.

And I was still there.

“Getting ready.”

Optimizing.

Fixing my method.

Telling myself — once I figure out the perfect system, I’ll be unstoppable.

Two months before the exam…

I just slowly stopped showing up.

No dramatic moment.

I just… didn’t go one day.

Then the next.

Then the next.

I told everyone I was still studying.

But honestly?

I was tired in a way that didn’t feel physical.

And I was scared.

Because if I gave it everything  
and still failed…

then maybe I’m not who I think I am.

So I left early.

That’s my pattern.

Start strong.

Disappear quietly.

People think I read a lot.

People think I know things.

Sometimes I feel like I just know how to talk about things.

And that’s a weird thing to admit.

This channel isn’t me trying to prove I’m smart.

It’s me trying to not disappear again.

When things get uncomfortable.

When it gets heavy.

Because I’m tired of restarting my life every year.

And honestly…

I think the younger version of me deserves at least one version of me that stays.

So I’m staying.

Let’s see what happens.

# 🎯 Delivery Principles (Read This Before Recording)

### 1. Underplay, don’t overperform

If it feels dramatic to you, it will feel fake on camera.

Aim for **honest, slightly tired, reflective**.

---

### 2. Silence is your strongest tool

Your pauses are doing half the work.

Don’t rush them.

Especially after:

- “I wasn’t lazy.”
    
- “I just slowly stopped showing up.”
    
- “then maybe I’m not who I think I am.”
    

---

### 3. Eye contact = trust

- Look away during thinking moments
    
- Look _directly_ at camera during truth moments
    

That contrast builds connection.

---

### 4. One rule

If a line feels like acting…

cut the performance in half.

# 🎯 PERFORMANCE CHEAT SHEET

### 1. Don’t “act emotional”

If you _try_ to sound deep, it will feel fake.

Instead:  
→ Speak like you’re figuring this out while talking.

---

### 2. Where to pause (critical)

Pause after:

- “I have a problem.”
    
- “It’s something worse.”
    
- “I just… slowly stopped showing up.”
    
- “if I’m actually enough.”
    
- “I don’t stay.”
    

These pauses create weight.

---

### 3. Eye Contact Strategy

- Truth → look directly at camera
    
- Thinking → look away
    
- Shame → slight downward glance
    

This alone builds trust.

---

### 4. Speed Control

- Lists = faster
    
- Truth = slower
    
- Confession = quiet + slower
    

---

### 5. Most Important Line

Deliver this one perfectly:

> “I think I leave early… so I never have to find out if I’m actually enough.”

Say it slower than you think.  
Almost like you don’t want to say it.
