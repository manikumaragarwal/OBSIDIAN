### How to make "High Retention Videos"

framework for creating viral UGC videos.
==**STEAL**== : Steal other viral videos.

secret: 90% of "**creative**" videos are just stealing other viral videos, with **_just 1 twist_**.

**STEP 1** : find a video that got 100K+ videos ==in your Niche==.
(_find a niche... or the way you make videos_)

**STEP 2** : Study their script ==sentence by sentence==

**Step 3** : Nothing is by accident when it comes to virality.
==**Why did this go VIRAL ????**==

> [!TIP] Creativity Science Backing
> This "steal with 1 twist" approach is backed by the neuroscience in [[OUTWORK everyone by being BORED]]:
>
> - The **Default Mode Network** (DMN) is where you make novel associations — the "1 twist"
> - You cannot make that twist by mindlessly scrolling; you need the mental space boredom creates
> - True creativity isn't random — it emerges from deep pattern recognition + space to recombine
