1. Retroactively trying to fix my self-esteem issues by being overly kind to someone else, because no one else was kind to me, so this is a way to being kind to my old self. 
2. Sometimes, I feel like I was born with a leak, and all the little talent and goodness I started with, slowly spilled out, and it's all gone. **Life is a series of closing doors**.
3. The problem with life is, *You either know what you want, but you don't get what you want*  OR  *you either get what you want, but now you don't know what you want.*

> [!NOTE] Literary Echo
> Thought #2 mirrors [[Doctor Faustus - Christopher Marlowe|Faustus's]] final monologue — the horror of wasted potential, the closing of all doors. In [[Aristotle's Poetics|Aristotelian terms]], this is the *Catharsis* moment — witnessing a character squander their gifts creates both pity and recognition.
> 
> Thought #3 maps to [[PRIDE AND PREJUDICE|Pride & Prejudice's]] central irony: Mrs. Bennet knows what she wants (marriages for her daughters) but cannot get it; Elizabeth doesn't know she wants Darcy until she almost loses him.
