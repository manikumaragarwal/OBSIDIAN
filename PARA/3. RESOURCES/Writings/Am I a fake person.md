Considering the things I do, considering the kind of lies I've built around in my life, I feel like i'm a fake person, but this belief is not accurate. 

A fake person lies to gain power or pleasure.  
I lied to **avoid humiliation, loss, and rejection**.

I know this doesn't make my behaviour harmless,
But this makes my intent *Human*

I was not trying to be *superior* when I lied.
I was just trying not to be *unlovable*.

> [!NOTE] Connection to Personal Brand
> [[Building your Personal Brand - Personal Notes|Brand theory]] says: "Share not only your wins, but your losses." This is that note. The authentic self that feels fake is actually *the most relatable* version — because the audience recognises it.
