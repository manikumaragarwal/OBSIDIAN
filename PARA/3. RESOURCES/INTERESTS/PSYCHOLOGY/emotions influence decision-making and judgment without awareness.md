# emotions influence decision-making and judgment without awareness

## Core Idea
Emotions constantly affect our judgments and decision-making, but we're often completely unaware of this influence.

## The Teacher Grading Study
A revealing experiment:
- Teachers were randomly assigned to either yellow (pleasant) or blue (unpleasant) mood states
- They then graded the same middle school essay
- **Result**: 1-2 full grade differences between the two groups
- **Key Finding**: 90% of teachers denied their emotions influenced their grading, despite clear data showing otherwise

## The Implication
- Emotions shape our perceptions and evaluations unconsciously
- We believe we're being objective when we're actually being emotionally influenced
- This affects everything from academic grading to workplace evaluations to personal relationships

## Mood Effects on Thinking
- **Negative moods** → narrow, critical thinking
- **Positive moods** → expansive, accepting thinking

## Broader Applications
- Judges sentencing decisions
- Managers evaluating employees
- Parents assessing children's behavior
- All influenced by emotional state without awareness
