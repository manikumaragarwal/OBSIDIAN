# the meta-moment tool for self-regulation

## What It Is
A 6-step process for managing emotional hijacks and responding from your best self rather than reacting impulsively.

## The Six Steps

### Step 1: Something Happens (Trigger)
- Awareness of personal triggers
- Small things that set us off (promises broken, minor annoyances)
- Big things that activate strong emotions

### Step 2: Emotional Hijack
- Recognizing when emotions have taken over
- When angry: everything becomes a grievance (digging up past offenses)
- When anxious: obsessive thinking takes over
- The amygdala has hijacked the prefrontal cortex

### Step 3: Breathe
- Activate the parasympathetic nervous system
- Take a conscious breath to calm the hijacked state
- Bring the prefrontal cortex back online for rational thinking
- The breath creates space between trigger and response

### Step 4: See Your Best Self
- Visualization of your ideal response
- What would the "feelings master" do?
- Creating motivation to regulate rather than just breathe
- Example: Mark Brackett's "feelings master" persona

### Step 5: Strategize
- Choose adaptive strategies over maladaptive ones
- Move from "I'm going to rip your head off" to constructive approaches
- Select from multiple regulation techniques

### Step 6: Problem Solve
- Take perspective of others involved
- Move from "me" to "we" and "us"
- Find solutions that work for everyone
- Accept that sometimes strategies fail, and that's okay

## Key Insight
"If you take the six steps seriously, you can avoid the 12 steps later" (referring to addiction recovery programs)

## The Reality
- Even experts struggle with this
- Mark Brackett admits: "Sometimes I take that breath... and I say to myself, wow, how am I going to just rip your head off?"
- You never regret taking a moment to be your best self
- You always regret being disregulated
