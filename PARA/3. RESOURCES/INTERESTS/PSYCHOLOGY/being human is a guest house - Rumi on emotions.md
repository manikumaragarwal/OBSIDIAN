# being human is a guest house - Rumi on emotions

## The Poem
> This being human is a guest house.
> Every morning a new arrival.
> A joy, a depression, a meanness,
> some momentary awareness comes
> as an unexpected visitor.
> Welcome and entertain them all!
> Even if they are a crowd of sorrows,
> who violently sweep your house
> empty of its furniture,
> still, treat each guest honorably.
> He may be clearing you out
> for some new delight.
> The dark thought, the shame, the malice,
> meet them at the door laughing
> and invite them in.
> Be grateful for whatever comes,
> because each has been sent
> as a guide from beyond.
> — Rumi

## The Message
All emotions are valid visitors in our experience:
- Joy, depression, meanness—all arrive unbidden
- Even difficult emotions may be preparing us for something new
- We should welcome rather than resist all emotional experiences
- Each emotion has something to teach us

## Connection to Emotional Intelligence
This poem captures the essence of EI:
- All emotions are valid and important
- It's what you do with them that matters
- Resistance creates suffering; acceptance creates wisdom
- Even anger can be useful when converted to passion

## The Cultural Bias Against "Negative" Emotions
- Society promotes constant happiness as the ideal
- This poem challenges that bias
- Sadness, anger, fear—all have value and purpose
- The emotionally intelligent person welcomes all experiences
