# Dan Harmon's Story Circle
> *A character can only change after paying the devastating price of confusing what they want with what they need.*

## What It Is
Dan Harmon's 8-step circular narrative structure, a streamlined, prescriptive adaptation of the Hero's Journey built for serial storytelling (TV and YouTube). The steps are: You, Need, Go, Search, Find, Pay, Return, Change. 

## Why It Works (The Psychology)
It uses a circle because the character ends exactly where they started, but fundamentally transformed. Psychologically, it hinges on the friction between conscious desire (Want) and subconscious necessity (Need). Growth only happens when the ego is battered; the character goes after what they *want*, but the universe forces them to confront what they *need*, extracting a heavy price in the process.

## How It Works in Practice
1. **You:** A character is in a zone of comfort.
2. **Need:** They want something.
3. **Go:** They enter an unfamiliar situation.
4. **Search:** They adapt to it.
5. **Find:** They get what they wanted.
6. **Pay:** They pay a heavy price for it.
7. **Return:** They return to their familiar situation.
8. **Change:** They have fundamentally changed.

## Movie / TV Examples
- **Rick and Morty:** Every episode features characters leaving domestic life (Go), doing something insane to get what they want (Find), suffering existential trauma because of it (Pay), and coming home to sit on the couch, slightly more broken or wiser (Change).
- **Community:** Abed wants to prove a point (Need), manipulates the group (Search), succeeds (Find), but realizes he alienated his friends (Pay), forcing him to apologize (Change).

## YouTube / Content Examples
- **Educational/Science Channels:** (e.g., Veritasium, Kurzgesagt, CGP Grey) The creator poses a question/problem (Go), dives into the complex research (Search), finds an answer (Find), but reveals that this answer dismantles a deeply held assumption of the audience (Pay), leaving everyone viewing the world differently (Change).
- **Challenge Videos:** A creator attempts a 30-day challenge (Need/Go), struggles but finishes (Find), realizes the physical or mental toll it took (Pay), and ends the video with a nuanced takeaway rather than blind celebration (Change).

## Common Beginner Mistakes
- **Skipping the "Pay" step:** Characters get what they want without a sacrifice. Without the heavy price, the change feels unearned and fake.
- **Ignoring the circle:** The character doesn't return to a familiar baseline to demonstrate the contrast between who they were and who they are now.

## Exercises to Practice
- Map out your favorite 10-minute YouTube video using the 8 steps. 
- Take a character's clear "Want" and brainstorm three painful "Needs" they might actually have to confront.
