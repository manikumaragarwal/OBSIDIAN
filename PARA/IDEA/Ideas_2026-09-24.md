Kabhi kabhi koi game aisa hota hai na...

jise khelne ke liye tumhe koi reason nahi chahiye.

Bas game kholo...
aur thodi der uski duniya mein reh lo.

**Sky: Children of the Light** bilkul waisa hi game hai.

Isme tum ek chhote se character ke saath ek huge, beautiful world explore karte ho...

clouds ke beech fly karte ho,
hidden places discover karte ho,
aur random logon se milte ho.

Aur honestly...

is game ki sabse crazy cheez iska gameplay bhi nahi hai.

It's the **feeling**.

Iski music...
yeh empty, peaceful landscapes...
aur bina kuch bole kisi random player ke saath fly karna...

pata nahi kyun, but it gives you that weird nostalgic feeling.

Jaise bachpan mein koi game khela tha,
jisme tumhe kuch achieve nahi karna tha...

bas ghoomna tha.

Aur shayad isi liye log saalon baad bhi iss game pe wapas aate hain.

Play Store pe bhi players baar-baar iski graphics, music, story aur community ki baat karte hain.

Haan, game perfect nahi hai.

Grinding hai,
kuch cosmetics kaafi expensive hain,

but phir bhi log isse chhod nahi paate.

Because **Sky isn't really a game you rush through.**

It's a game you...
just spend time in.

Aur honestly,
aajkal ke 15-second dopamine wale games ke beech...

thoda slow hona bhi accha lagta hai.
