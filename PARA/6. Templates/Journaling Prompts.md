# Journaling Prompts

> There's no right way to do this. No pressure, no structure you have to follow. Just write whatever feels true.

---

## The Basics

- What's on your mind right now?
- What's something you noticed today — anything at all?
- How are you feeling about things at the moment?

## When You Want to Go Deeper

- What's a thought that keeps circling back to you?
- Is there anything you want to explore more?
- What's something you've been avoiding?
- What would you tell yourself a year from now?

## For Days When Words Don't Come Easy

- If you're not sure what to write, just start with: "Honestly, I don't really know what to say, but..."
- Or just describe the room you're in. What do you see? What do you hear?
- Write a single sentence about how your body feels right now.
